## Correction (made after the first pass)

The first version of this review scored only `Rain - Seed 1`, `Rain - Seed 2` and
`Why I Quit`. There are four more seed documents, and reading them reverses two
verdicts and adds three losses the first pass missed.

**Shiori is not scaffolding.** She has a 643-word seed of her own. She is the love
interest, drawn from Marie in Persona 4 Golden, and she is explicitly *"a character
who simply does the thing I want to be able to do the most, to actually create things
without so much worry about the result."* That is the carefree-making thread the first
pass listed as missing from all fifty cards — it isn't missing, it is **her**, and the
Spine doesn't use it.

What the Spine does with her instead: a vault breach, a device mix-up, an unsourced
laugh, and footage that exonerates Rain. She has been reduced from the emotional answer
of the book to a witness in a legal technicality. Her seed material — the making without
fear, the bookmarking of moments, the memory problem, the mutual-vulnerability
loneliness — appears essentially nowhere.

So: **cut the Gadget X / Nimbus legal fiction, keep and considerably expand Shiori.**
Those were one recommendation in the first pass. They are opposite recommendations.

**Vesper lost his two sharpest features.** The seed gives him the ability to extract
*decision memories* — memories where a conscious choice was made. The Spine generalises
this to "autobiographical memory-network," which is blander and, more importantly, off
the book's own theme: this is a story about committing to decisions, and the seed's
villain steals decisions specifically. The seed also has him forgetting recent events
after each extraction, and starting inside the party before betraying it. Neither is in
the Spine, which installs him as asylum director from his first appearance.

## What to consider cutting

**Outright (6 cards):** Arc 1 card 5; Arc 2 cards 3 and 7; Arc 4 cards 1 and 3;
Arc 5 card 1. Six transit or machinery cards, 50 → 44.

**The two invented plots, demoted rather than deleted:**

1. **The theft mix-up** (Nimbus / Gadget X). Its only job is to make the accusation

   **Not Shiori.** She stays. See the correction above.

2. **Aster and the Pursuer.** Aster gives Roxana a motive she doesn't need; Seed 1 gives
   her one already (conviction, isolation, nobody to lean on). The Pursuer gives the story

**The clue system (TELL 1/2/3).** A mystery scaffold laid over a story whose tension is
not a mystery. Nothing in either seed is about figuring something out.

## What to put back

- **Claire shooting bullets out of the air.** Her seed says the character was built
  around this as her *introduction*. It belongs at Arc 1 card 6, where she enters.
- **Vesper taking decision memories, not memories in general** (S20). This single
  change puts the antagonist back on the book's actual theme. Everything downstream —
  the Melt, the extraction, the void's re-weighting — gets sharper, because what he
  takes is the thing Rain is most afraid to make.
- **Vesper forgetting after each extraction** (S21) — a cost the Spine gave him none of.
- **Shiori making things without fear of the result** (S17), and bookmarking moments
  against her own memory problem (S18). This is the answer the book is arguing toward,
  embodied in a person, and it is currently unused.
- **Roxana's tone-deaf advice** (S23) — "have you tried not scratching it?" A specific,
  lived, immediately writable scene about offered help that lands as dismissal. It is
  the setup that makes her accepting the Ghost's hand in Arc 3 mean something.
- **The commute as an identity fact** (S14 — commuting is why he was the outsider).
  Arc 1 opens on a commute and Dead End 4 closes on one, but neither card knows why
  that image matters to you.
- **The art class** (S11) — rigidity, the prescribed assignment, not realising it could
  have been anything. That is the Asylum's argument in miniature, and it's lived.
- **Carefree making** (S13 — Duel Disks, dev logs). Rain is only ever shown *not*
  creating. Nothing in 50 cards shows him having once made something gladly, which is
  what the loss is supposed to be measured against.

## On "The drift" specifically

Your note — that it should show Rain's identity pulling him back, and show more of him —
is right, and it's a sharper reading than the card has.

What the seed supports: the hiding becomes the commute. The same ceiling, the same
"not yet," the same fridge inventory, in a different room. He builds a new routine
inside the emergency and it comforts him. That is S5, S6 and S14 at once, and it
rhymes deliberately with card 0.

What to drop: "PITFALL — go home" (a system label, not a scene) and the Pursuer
glimpse (a different plot's errand).

What is genuinely open and yours to decide: **what is the "not yet" in this room?**
In card 0 it was the story outline he wouldn't open. Here it is something else, and
nothing in the package decides it for you.

### All four settled

1. **Vesper takes decision memories** (S20).
2. **Arc 2 keeps two residents** — the Fearless One and the Ruined One.
3. **The theft mix-up is cut.** Gadget X and Nimbus's two-object bookkeeping go. The

4. **Aster is cut.** He resolved the loneliness Roxana's seed needs left open, converted
   her arc from receiving help into searching for a person, and replaced her own small
   flame with an inherited one.
