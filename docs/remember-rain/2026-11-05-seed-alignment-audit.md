## 2. Four things were still open and mechanical — all four written

### (a) `Arc 5 · 10 The outbound train` said the wrong last line

The brief read **"In spite of it all, I choose to be Rain."** Rain's locked
callbacks, Spine card 10 (which instructs *use the exact selected callback*), and the
Rain seed's own closing line (§13) all say **"I am Rain."** This brief was the only
document in the project still disagreeing with the author's own sentence.

Now reads: end on the exact locked callback, with the correction dated in place.

### (b) `World/Magic System` contradicted itself ten lines apart

The Extraction section opened "Vesper extracts a coherent **autobiographical** memory
or connected memory-network through direct contact" — then the taxonomy below it
states decision memory is "the only kind his hands take." The opening line predates
the 2026-11-05 restoration and was never updated.

Now: "a coherent **decision memory** or connected network of decisions," pointing at
the taxonomy. 3,462 → 3,480 words.

### (c) Vesper's consent-at-scale seam had no resolution anywhere

His sheet says he **"asks permission every time and means it."** The city was never
asked. `Arc 4 · 5` has Claire saying honestly that "the correction asks permission,"
while `Magic System` delivers it through the water supply.

The answer was already in `World/Locations` and had not been written down anywhere it
would be read: the Ministry's register *is* the asking — *Circulation Adjustment
Notice*, *Resonance Compliance Renewal*, *Household Flow Assessment*. At city scale
his one consistent virtue does not lapse, it industrialises. The notice went out and
it was compliant. Claire is not lying and is not right; she is repeating a true
sentence about a form.

Written into `Characters/1 Vesper` as **At city scale, asking becomes a form**, with a
cross-reference on the permission bullet and a new Do Not Flatten line ("Do not let
the city correction read as the moment he abandoned consent… the horror is the
continuity, not a lapse"). 1,676 → 1,872 words. The `{TK}` for *where that scene
lands* is carried in the sheet — `Arc 2 · 6` and `Arc 4 · 5` are the candidates.

### (d) `Arc Structure` carried the arc ladder as handoffs, not as questions

The Rain seed's §8 gives each arc an establishment **and** an unresolved question that
the next arc takes up — that ladder is the stated reason the five arcs do not repeat
one lesson in escalating circumstances. `Arc Structure`'s Handoff column was
logistical ("The apparent refuge is pleasant and open-doored"), so the philosophical
chain lived only in the seed.

Added **What Each Arc Leaves Unresolved** as a five-row table, plus two reconciliation
notes. 362 → 743 words. The author's existing rows were not overwritten.

- **Arc 3.** Canon's development line is "Possibility itself has value"; the seed's

  reality he actually inhabits." These are the two halves of the same beat — canon
  names what the ending costs, the seed names what it buys. Both recorded.

  you?"; the seed asks "Can I take initiative when I still feel the same hesitation?"
  Rain's sheet has him "move with sudden commitment in emergencies," so removing the
  emergency *is* the sharper staging of the seed's question. Canon stays.

## 6. Two things in the full Vesper seed that canon did not have

Both confirmed absent by grep across the live binder **and** the trash, then written.

### (a) §4's alternative: reframing, which is not a power

The seed names the thing Vesper never does:

> "What if the better approach were to help someone recognize what is hurting them? To
> help them confront that experience, find ways to mitigate the damage, or reframe what
> it means for their life? **Reframing the meaning of an experience is not necessarily
> lying to yourself.** Something terrible can remain terrible. The facts do not need to
> change. But a person can still decide what those facts will mean."

Canon had the *mechanical* version of this — "Removal is his only offer," and "Shatter
is the exception, and he does not use it" — but both frame the alternative as a
Deviation he withholds. The seed's point is sharper: the better help **requires no
ability at all.** The only hits for "reframe" anywhere in the package were four trashed
documents, and all four use it in the opposite sense (he reframes a patient's
resistance as confusion — *"You're not ready yet."*).

Written into `Characters/1 Vesper` as **The alternative he never considers is not a
power** — he has read enough lives to do it better than almost anyone, he never offers
it because removal is the help he can verify, and a person still carrying something is a
result he cannot confirm. The last clause closes §2's "terrible irony" and §10's third
open question in the same sentence: he withholds it from himself on the same grounds,
understanding his mother's departure perfectly and never once deciding what it is
allowed to mean. 1,872 → 1,992 words.

The authorial half went into `Story Plan/2 Pessimism of Strength`'s **Care and
subtraction**: care can change what an experience means without changing what happened,
and it is the only form of help that leaves the person still holding their own past.

### (b) §5's guardrail: stillness that was chosen

> "A quiet, comfortable life is not inherently wrong. Someone who genuinely chooses that
> life is not necessarily stagnant. **The difference is between choosing to remain where
> you are and losing the ability to choose anything else.**"

Zero hits in the package for any form of this. `Pessimism of Strength` carried the
relief half — "Relief that restores that ability is medicine" — and nothing protecting
the person who simply chooses to stay. That matters beyond Vesper: without it, the
asylum residents and the Ruined One read as indicted by the book rather than as people
it declines to judge, and the Rain seed's §14 asks for exactly this guard — *"I don't
want to turn the philosophy into a demand that everyone must constantly take
initiative."*

Added as a short section, **Stillness that was chosen**: the distinction defended is
between choosing to remain and losing the ability to choose otherwise, never between
moving and staying; nothing licenses measuring a person by how much they initiate.
346 → 504 words.
