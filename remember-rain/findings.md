# Findings register — Remember Rain

Every finding raised in this space, with its current status. Findings are **added
and superseded, never deleted** — a register that quietly loses entries cannot be
trusted to catch drift over time, which is the whole reason it exists.

**This file is the index and the decision queue. The evidence is not duplicated
here.** Full findings — with `file:line` citations, exact quotes, the
Observation / Inference / Proposal split, candidate resolutions, and
Scrivener-ready replacement text — live in the dated report under `audits/`.

**ID prefixes:** the letter records which audit raised the finding, so IDs stay
unique across the register without renumbering archived evidence.
`F-` = terminology / canon audit. `E-` = element-vision alignment audit.

**Status values:** `open` · `author-decided (date)` · `dissolved (D-xxx)`

A finding is only closed by the author, and only in `decisions.md`.

## Correction to an earlier claim in this register

An earlier version of this file described the sidecars as "a second canon layer"
and called the 62 synopses "load-bearing." **That was wrong**, and D-001 corrects
it. The synopses are drafting notes — example descriptions of scenes, not
statements of canon. The reasoning error is worth recording, because it is a trap
this space could fall into again:

> 62 of 80 sidecars carrying text found in no `.md` file is a *statistical*
> observation. Nothing about it establishes authority. The text was unseen, and
> unseen was read as load-bearing. Volume is not weight.

The genuine lesson that survives is narrower and still useful: **the sidecars
were invisible, and invisible meant unaudited.** They are now a known quantity.
The finding that came out of them worth keeping is not a contradiction at all —
it is that the notes contain named ideas ("Still Point," "the Unburdening," "the
appointment") which the plan has never mentioned. Under D-001 those are simply
notes, and no action follows. Mentioned only so the author knows they exist.

## Where the 43 live findings cluster

Not 43 separate problems — two patterns, and one of them has a single fix.

**1. The world-rules layer is thin relative to what the plot asks of it.**
`World/Magic System.md` is 1,749 words and carries five arcs. Correction appears
in it exactly once, as an open question rather than a definition
(`World/Magic System.md:17`) — yet correction is Arc 4's clock, Arc 5's
objective, and the horror engine for the whole back half (E-001). Costs get one
line (`:55`, "Cost scales with use and cannot be bypassed") while the Arc 3
escape depends on an unpriced transfer (E-004). Passive ownership gets one clause
(`:25`, "the owner is the only person who directly perceives it") that the Arc 3
escape appears to violate (E-003).

The highest-leverage single action in this register is **one tightening pass on
`World/Magic System.md`.** It is plausibly the fix for E-001, E-002, E-003,
E-004, E-009, E-010, E-012, E-029, F-003, F-007, F-008, and F-017 — twelve
findings across both audits, all reachable from one document. (F-008 was
dissolved by D-001, so eleven remain reachable; the cluster is unchanged.)
Sequence matters: several scene-level findings resolve themselves once the rules
are stated, so ruling on the mechanic layer first shrinks the queue rather than
growing it.

**2. Scenes that promise to *show* something and supply nothing that shows it.**
E-021 (the demonstration meant to show Vesper's kindness), E-023 (two residents
carrying Arc 2's and Arc 4's central arguments, neither with a document), E-018
(three characters spending themselves with no stated reason), E-025 (Rain's
defining unpredictability stated as an effect on someone's model and given no
action). These are the plan asserting an effect on the reader rather than
building the detail that produces it. They need invention, which is the author's,
not the space's.

**A third point is genuinely reassuring:** the arithmetic and the structure hold.
Vesper's age, the Widower's nine treatments, the story's duration, arc and
pitfall counts, scene numbering, the locked callbacks, and the Pursuer's fuel
spend all check out — and the element audit's "elements that pass" section
separately clears the Widower, the hand motif, Rain's trait pair, and Arc 3's
completion mechanism. The outline's *scaffolding* is sound; what is missing is
the specification underneath it.
