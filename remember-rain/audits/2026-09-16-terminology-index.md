# Remember Rain — Terminology Index

Companion to `terminology.md`. Every named thing found in the corpus, where it is defined, where
else it is used, and the verdict. Citations are `path/relative/to/novel:LINE`. `MetaData.txt`
entries are Scrivener sidecars — unpublished, and the source of several verdicts below.

**Verdict key:** `consistent` — defined once, used the same way everywhere · `drifting` — one concept
under several names, or one name meaning two things · `conflicting` — two documents cannot both be
true · `undefined` — the story depends on it and no document specifies it · `status` — provisional
material treated as settled, or vice versa.
