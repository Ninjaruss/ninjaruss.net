# Worldbuilding integration options — Remember Rain

**Date:** 2026-09-29 · **Status:** proposal. Nothing here is canon and nothing has
been applied. No finding has been appended to `findings.md` — the candidates in
Part 5 are offered for the author to promote or decline.

**The question this answers:** where the World layer could carry more of what the
story is already built to test, and what each option would cost elsewhere.

**Method note.** Every option is priced against the story's own instruments, not
against convention: the hierarchy claims in `Story Plan/0 What is Remember
Rain.md`, the motif families in `Story Plan/Themes and Motifs.md`, and the
rubric in `Story Plan/2 Pessimism of Strength.md`. Where an option would breach a
document's own stated constraint, that constraint is quoted and the breach is
named. Where an option is one I recommend *against*, it is listed with reasons
drawn from the documents rather than from taste.

**Register key, used throughout:** *Observation* (what the text literally says) ·
*Inference* (what follows from it) · *Option* (what could be done, with its cost).

**Not in scope, deliberately:** F-006, F-007, F-011, F-012, F-016, F-019, F-020,
F-022, F-024, E-001, E-017, E-019, and everything already held open in
`Story Plan/Open Questions.md`. Where an option here touches one of those, it
says so instead of re-raising it.

## Part 1 — What the World layer owns, and the boundaries it sets itself

*Observation.* Three documents, each carrying an explicit statement of its own
job and limits:

| Document | Owns | Declares as its limit |
|---|---|---|
| `World/Magic System.md` (183 lines) | The resonance taxonomy: Flare, passive, Pull, Imprint, Deviation, costs, extraction, glass, correction | `:3` "The system should stay legible through objects, bodily costs, and repeated behavior rather than exposition." · `:183` "Keep emotional or arc interpretation in Character and Story Plan references." |
| `World/History.md` (41 lines) | Chronology and institutions across the five years | `:41` "Keep locations, shared mechanics, and character arcs in their respective references." |
| `World/Locations.md` (75 lines) | Place anchors | `:3` "Use these as scene anchors, not as a complete geography." · `:11` palette "should not resolve into a recognizable real-world culture" · `:21` "Avoid overt dystopian spectacle." · `:45` "Never give the Ministry a single villainous face." |

*Inference.* These limits are not incidental. Three of the options below are
attractive precisely because they would cross one of them, and the crossing is
the cost. Two of them have **no legal home at all**, which is itself the finding
in Cluster A.

## Part 2 — What the story now asks of the World layer

*Observation.* The plan layer is a single dated snapshot (all 19 non-manuscript
documents, 2026-08-28). Since then, rulings D-003, D-004, D-006 and D-007 moved
weight onto three things that the World layer does not own:

1. **Wanting.** D-003 made correction act on it (`World/Magic System.md:81`), but
   three separate mechanisms now act on it and it is defined nowhere —
   including anywhere in the terminology index, which has no row for it.
2. **The civic ordinary.** Correction's horror is specified as arriving through
   ordinary administration (`Story Plan/Themes and Motifs.md:181` — "Evil arrives
   through offers, forms, referrals, renovations, and schedules"), and the
   Ministry's vocabulary is written (`World/Locations.md:37`–`:43`) — but the
   World layer has no anchor for where a person meets it.
3. **The recording layer.** `Characters/Shiori.md:81` scales her confiscated
   recorder into "unattended surveillance infrastructure"; the index records
   that Shiori's rig is "`World/Locations.md` (not covered)"; and the newest
   prose now puts a camera on the page (`Manuscript/Arc 1 - Fugitive/2 The
   vault.md:207`).

*Observation, recency.* The newest manuscript scene is
`Manuscript/Arc 1 - Fugitive/2 The vault.md` (sidecar `Modified: September 14,
2026 at 11:03 PM`), which post-dates every plan document by two weeks. Under the
charter's rule, that prose outranks the plan. It has already built things the
World layer does not name — see Cluster D.

## Cluster A — The world's central rule and the story's central claim are the same proposition, and the coupling has no home

*Observation.*

- `World/Magic System.md:13` — "Irreversible transitions do not generate fresh
  resonance. Under sufficient exposure, they pattern resonance already present in
  the person, object, or environment."
- `World/Magic System.md:145` — "Magic is not a collectible power set. It is a
  record of irreversible transition, a person's relationship to that record, and
  a cost paid through the system being used."
- `Story Plan/0 What is Remember Rain.md:11` — "**Becoming is unavoidable.**
  People are embodied, limited, conditioned, and continually changed by reality;
  nobody can remain a perfectly preserved possibility."
- `Story Plan/2 Pessimism of Strength.md:17` — "Strength is not domination,
  productivity, invulnerability, or the ability to tolerate unlimited suffering.
  It is the capacity to encounter reality, be changed by it, and still make a
  deliberate response."
- Every cast origin is exactly one such transition: Rain's exit
  (`Characters/0 Rain.md:21` — "Under sufficient local resonance, that exit
  patterned the personal resonance he later experiences as the Ghost"), Vesper's
  abandonment (`Characters/1 Vesper.md:41`), Claire's collapse
  (`Characters/Claire.md:75`), Roxana's fire (`Characters/Roxana.md:41`), the
  Pursuer's choice of work (`Characters/The Pursuer.md:43`), Shiori's injury
  (`Characters/Shiori.md:19`).

*Inference.* The world rule and the story's central claim are the same assertion
in two vocabularies: a transition does not create a self, it patterns what was
already there — and becoming cannot be avoided or preserved. No document states
this relation. `Story Plan/Themes and Motifs.md` never cites `World/Magic
System.md` on it, and the coupling is currently accidental rather than chosen.

*The structural problem.* There is no document where this could legally be said.
`World/Magic System.md:183` forbids interpretation in that file; `World/History.md:41`
pushes character arcs out; `Story Plan/Themes and Motifs.md` is an instrument for
images, contrasts, and carriers, not for propositions about the world. So the
strongest available integration between the world and the theme is homeless.

**Options**

- **A1 · Leave it uncoupled.** The reader who assembles it gets it; nothing is
  added. *Cost:* none. The story keeps its refusal to explain, which
  `Themes and Motifs.md:183` actively wants ("Prefer missing speeches to
  translated symbolism").
- **A2 · State the coupling in `Story Plan/Themes and Motifs.md`.** *Cost:* it
  creates a second home for a world rule — the exact drift this space exists to
  catch — and it converts a reader-assembled recognition into a stated thesis,
  against `:183`.
- **A3 · Adopt it as a selection rule, phrased mechanically.** Add to
  `World/Magic System.md`'s Constraints section (`:163`–`:183`) the rule that
  every passive and Imprint must be the legible shape of a transition that
  actually occurred to that person or object, and may never be a capability that
  could be collected, transferred, or upgraded. This is a mechanics constraint,
  so `:183` does not bar it, and `:3`'s "legible through objects, bodily costs,
  and repeated behavior" is exactly what it enforces. *Cost:* it binds future
  invention, and it would retro-actively discipline anything already drifting —
  it is adjacent to F-017 ("borrowed loadout") without deciding it.

*Open question for the author:* is the overlap between the resonance rule and the
story's claim about becoming something the story wants to be *deliberate*, or is
it the happy accident it currently reads as and should be left alone?

*Status impact:* A3 would change `World/Magic System.md` from "current canon"
mechanics into mechanics plus a constraint on invention.

## Cluster B — "Wanting" is the undefined object of three documented operations

*Observation.* "Wanting" is acted on by three separate systems, with three
different verbs, and is defined in no World document:

| Operation | Where | What it does to wanting |
|---|---|---|
| Correction | `World/Magic System.md:81` | "what a corrected district loses is wanting in general — the appetite for a thing rather than the record of it" |
| Correction, graded | `World/Magic System.md:87`, `:91` | "Exposure degrades wanting in proportion to time and concentration" · "can still say what happened and cannot say what they want" |
| Furnace | `World/Magic System.md:65` | "The Pursuer burns the wanting that once drove him." |
| Extraction | `World/Magic System.md:149` | "Vesper cannot extract a want as a separate object. He removes the memories and associations that sustain it." |

*Also:* `Story Plan/Arc Structure.md:13` states Arc 2's question as "Can you keep
wanting when wanting hurts?"; `Story Plan/1 The Spine.md:47` — "He offers relief
from the ache of wanting."; `Story Plan/Choice Points.md:43` — pitfall 2 protects
"the present from painful wanting"; `Story Plan/Themes and Motifs.md:169` —
"`unlit` — wanting removed."

*Observation, term inventory.* The 2026-09-16 terminology index contains no row
for "wanting." It was never audited as a term, so its absence of definition has
never been recorded.

*Inference.* Three operations are precisely specified and their shared object is
not. A reader can say exactly what Vesper does and exactly what the loop does,
and has no word for the thing both are done to. Since "wanting" is also the
word the arc questions and the gold vocabulary's `unlit` depends on, the
undefined term is load-bearing in four registers at once.

*Counter-argument, stated fairly.* `World/Magic System.md:25` establishes that
the world deliberately withholds public terms: "There is no public technical
term inside the story, so names such as the Ghost and Static are personal." An
unglossed "wanting" may be the same choice made a second time, and defining it
could be the error.

*Related but distinct:* Pull is already defined (`World/Magic System.md:27`, "the
interaction creates an unmistakable attraction"), and the newest prose plays Pull
and wanting together on purpose — `Manuscript/Arc 1 - Fugitive/2 The vault.md:77`
"it was clear something was pulling my mind here" against `:175` "But I \*want\*
the goggles." Two attraction-words sharing a metaphor is not drift here;
it is the scene doing the work. Any definition must therefore define wanting
*against* Pull rather than as Pull.

**Options**

- **B1 · Define wanting as a world term, explicitly distinguished from Pull.**
  State, in `### Costs` or a short adjacent subsection, that wanting is a
  person's appetite for a specific thing, that Pull is resonance-compatibility
  attraction toward an Imprint, and then give the three operations one line each
  against that object. *Cost:* it touches the closed vocabulary — `:169`'s
  `unlit` and `Themes and Motifs.md:161`'s `faded` both gloss wanting, so both
  would need re-reading — and it decides the question the counter-argument above
  leaves open.
- **B2 · Define the relation without defining the object.** State only how
  correction, extraction, and Furnace differ in *layer* (appetite thinned;
  sustaining memory removed; accumulated charge spent) and leave "wanting"
  itself unglossed. *Cost:* partial legibility; the world gains comparability
  between three mechanics without gaining a word for what the sufferer lost.
- **B3 · Leave it.** *Cost:* the three mechanics keep acting on an undocumented
  thing, and each of the story's four registers that names it keeps relying on a
  term the world does not own.

*Open question for the author:* is "wanting" unglossed on purpose — the world's
own version of a condition with no public term — or is it the missing axis the
three mechanics already imply?

*Status impact:* B1 or B2 would add to `World/Magic System.md` and would need
`Story Plan/Themes and Motifs.md:161`/`:169` checked for consistency.

## Cluster C — District, station, boundary: the three units of Arc 4's clock are all undefined

*Observation.* Arc 4's pressure is a moving line, and it is expressed in three
units none of which any document defines:

- The line: `World/Magic System.md:79` — "the loop does to a district at exposure
  range" · `:83` — "The boundary is the edge of the loop's current reach. It is
  measured rather than decreed."
- The unit of loss: `Story Plan/1 The Spine.md:85` — "The same stations appear
  twice; the boundary moves between passes. The rain thins in corrected
  districts." · `:89` — "The corrected boundary reaches Rain's parents' district.
  Their calls stop, and he initially mistakes the silence for relief."
- The reporting unit: `World/History.md:27` — "it reaches an anchor station only
  after the amnesty is refused." The terminology index already records that
  "Anchor station" is "undefined — never defined as a Circuit Line term."
- The appearance: `World/Locations.md:21` — "cleaner schedules, quieter
  districts, thinner weather, and fewer disruptions."

*Inference.* Arc 4's clock, its measurement, and its emotional beat all run on
"district," and a district has no stated nature: how many there are, whether they
are water zones or administrative areas, or whether the boundary is observed as
pressure, as paperwork, or as the second appearance of a station. The scene has
to make the units legible unaided, and Arc 5 then reuses the boundary as the
thing advancing below the climb (`Story Plan/1 The Spine.md:107`) with no shared
vocabulary to reuse.

*Note on overlap:* this sits beside F-012 (apparatus and eruption undefined) and
the indexed-but-unnumbered "anchor station." Districts themselves are in no
document and in no inventory row.

**Options**

- **C1 · Districts are water-supply zones.** The loop delivers to districts, so a
  district is a unit of both civic supply and correction reach, and stations are
  named for them. *Cost:* makes the boundary a plumbing fact — very close to
  `World/Locations.md:21`'s "cleaner schedules," but it risks the advance reading
  as maintenance rather than as loss.
- **C2 · Districts are administrative units.** The Ministry's offices are
  organised by district, and an anchor station is a district's terminal.
  *Cost:* the horror becomes civic paperwork, which serves
  `Themes and Motifs.md:181` — but it edges toward giving the Ministry an
  organisation chart, which `World/Locations.md:45` resists.
- **C3 · Define neither; let the scene carry the units by use.** The player learns
  a district by seeing the same station in two states. *Cost:* nothing is added to
  the World layer, but there is then no vocabulary for Arc 5's advance, for
  Arc 3 intake's administrative paper, or for Rain's parents' district as a
  place rather than a phrase.

*Open question for the author:* is correction *measured* by pressure or by
paperwork? The story says both are true — the water is the contact
(`World/Magic System.md:81`) and the horror arrives through forms
(`Themes and Motifs.md:181`) — but which one the boundary is read off decides
what Arc 4's clock actually looks like on screen.

*Status impact:* C1 or C2 would add a term to `World/Locations.md` (or
`World/History.md`) that three other documents already assume.

## Cluster D — The newest prose has already built an ordinary layer that no World document names

*Observation.* `Manuscript/Arc 1 - Fugitive/2 The vault.md` (post-plan,
2026-09-14) establishes, in the live edge of the work:

- a worker shed — `:35` "It seems to be a storage spot for workers to drop off
  their equipment… Thick, yellow rain jackets hang on a nearby rack. A large
  whiteboard, with poorly erased smudges and pinned papers, listing a bunch of
  charts and numbers."
- `:39`–`:41` — "Either these sheets were replaced recently, or nobody actually
  reads them." · the clipboard "looks like it's been handled a thousand times.
  Handwritten signatures and logs for a paper titled \"Daily Boiler and
  Circulation Log\". Clearly the thing that truly matters around here compared to
  the corporate drivel above."
- `:49`, `:199` — a boiler room and maintenance area; `:219` — "a series of
  maintenance tunnels"
- `:73` — confiscated objects as ordinary household goods: "keys, utility tools,
  articles of clothing… organized by ID numbers"
- `:199`, `:213`, `:219` — cyan duct tape marking Shiori's route
- `:159` — "SECURITY RECORDINGS WILL BE REVIEWED."
- `:207` — "A camera stared down at me above the doorway."

*The route matters.* In the prose the sequence is alley → worker shed → boiler
room → maintenance tunnels → the restricted warehouse holding the goggles. The
plan compresses the same transition to `World/Locations.md:55` — "The alley gives
the story its first compressed transition from familiar public movement to the
restricted Nimbus space." So the live edge has made **the Sacred Water loop's own
maintenance space the physical route into the state's confiscation storage.**

*Inference.* This is the corpus's strongest existing world–plot integration, and
it is unrecorded. The loop is (a) how Rain obtains the goggles, his Imprint, and
(b) the vehicle by which correction later reaches every district
(`World/Magic System.md:79`). The newest prose has already joined the story's
first theft to the story's final object, using the infrastructure the finale
destroys.

*Inference, craft.* The two details that carry the theme are both *objects* — the
pristine pages nobody reads against the log that has been handled a thousand
times, and the confiscated household goods filed by ID. That is
`World/Magic System.md:3` ("legible through objects, bodily costs, and repeated
behavior rather than exposition") and the Record/Evidence/Trace family
(`Themes and Motifs.md:31`) executed as worldbuilding rather than as comment. It
is the model the rest of the World layer could follow.

**Options**

- **D1 · Record the layer.** Add to `World/Locations.md` a short entry naming the
  working and maintenance surface of the loop — worker sheds, boiler rooms,
  maintenance tunnels, the circulation log — as the loop's ordinary face rather
  than as new geography. *Cost:* `World/Locations.md:3` declares it holds "scene
  anchors, not as a complete geography," so this crosses that posture. The
  mitigation is that the loop already has an entry (`:27`) and this is its
  surface, not a new place.
- **D2 · Leave it to the prose.** *Cost:* four later beats lean on the same layer
  — Arc 3 intake's logged tray (`Story Plan/1 The Spine.md:63`), Arc 4's
  boundary, Arc 5's hub (`World/Locations.md:73`), and the finale's collapse
  (`:25`) — and each would re-invent the vocabulary the vault scene already
  fixed.
- **D3 · Record it as the template for the two other unrecorded layers** (the
  household/assessment layer behind the Ministry's three form names in Cluster
  E's shadow, and the recording layer in Cluster H). *Cost:* the largest edit
  here. It changes `World/Locations.md`'s job from an anchor list to a layered
  city, which is a real change and needs the author's yes on D1 first.

*Open question for the author:* is `Locations.md` an anchor list or a city? The
document's own answer is "anchors." If that holds, these layers want a home
elsewhere — and there is currently nowhere else.

*Status impact:* D1 changes `World/Locations.md`'s stated scope at `:3`; that
line would need revising or the entry would sit against it.

## Cluster E — One institution, three open questions, and a fourth thing that depends on the answer

This is the highest-leverage cluster in this document: one decision resolves a
ruling's leftover, two author-flagged open questions, and the ownership of
half the cast's institutional context.

*Observation.* The same question is asked in three vocabularies and a fourth item
silently depends on it:

1. `Story Plan/Open Questions.md:7` — "Did the asylum predate Vesper? Is he its
   operator, public face, research subject, or something else?"
2. `Story Plan/Open Questions.md:11` — "What institution allowed a
   thirteen-to-eighteen-year-old Vesper to practice, and what did it believe it
   was authorizing?"
3. `remember-rain/decisions.md`, D-003 — "What this ruling does not decide: who
   operates the correction apparatus — Vesper alone, or the state with him as the
   engine."
4. `World/History.md:13` — "The island city, its dormant volcano, the water
   infrastructure, the Circuit Line, the Water Ministry, and Nimbus Co. all
   predate the supernatural… **The Pursuer's long institutional career also
   predates the Flare.**" History names its pre-Flare institutions — city,
   volcano, water infrastructure, Circuit Line, Water Ministry, Nimbus Co. — then
   asserts one more for the Pursuer and never names it.

*Observation, the bind.* Both named bodies are explicitly constrained away from
the function the Pursuer performs: `World/History.md:37` — "The Water Ministry's
institutional power grows from water administration and resonance management, not
from a separate supernatural government"; `:39` — "Nimbus remains a contractor
operating through Ministry contracts rather than a replacement state." But the
Pursuer's work needs a body that owns custody, records, and offers:

- `Characters/The Pursuer.md:3` — "A man who has served the same institution for
  thirty years"
- `:17` — "Thirty years of service supplied the heading"
- `:53` — "His signature appears on Aster's intake record."
- `:55` — "He presents a genuine amnesty"
- `Story Plan/1 The Spine.md:95` — "surrender the listed property, including the
  goggles, and the charges can be resolved"
- `Story Plan/1 The Spine.md:55` — "Rain is still kneeling when the transfer
  begins."
- `Story Plan/1 The Spine.md:35` and `Story Plan/Arc Structure.md:13` — "state
  custody" / "referred to the state"
- `World/History.md:23` — "Known extractions, Melts, investigations, and
  institutional cases fall within these five years."
- `Story Plan/Themes and Motifs.md:55` — "**Pursuer / Vesper:** one obeys process;
  the other takes control because process is too slow." Process needs an owner.

*Inference.* Items 1–3 are one question, and item 4 is a fourth face of it. If
the asylum's parent is the body that licensed Vesper and operates the apparatus,
then Arc 2's pleasant refuge, Arc 4's advancing boundary, and the Pursuer's
ledger are one institution seen at three altitudes — and the story's horror is
both the offer and the enforcement, which is what
`Themes and Motifs.md:181` already claims ("Evil arrives through offers, forms,
referrals, renovations, and schedules").

*Inference, the cost of that answer.* It would make Arc 2's shelter retrospectively
part of the thing that thins districts, which brushes against
`Characters/1 Vesper.md:37` — "The reveal changes the reader's understanding of
his role; it is not a mask dropping." It is compatible: nothing about the
asylum's pleasantness changes, only its scale, and Vesper's kindness stays real
before and after. But the brush is real and the author should want it.

**Options**

- **E1 · One body, three altitudes.** Answer all four with a single authority: it
  licensed Vesper, the asylum is its front or its licensed site, and the
  apparatus is its installation. *Cost:* promotes author-flagged open questions to
  canon, closes D-003's residue, and requires a change to `World/History.md`'s
  institution list — which is currently the document that says there is no
  separate supernatural government (`:37`). Resolving it as *a function the
  Ministry holds* rather than a new body would honour `:37`; it would then need
  `World/Locations.md:45`'s "never give the Ministry a single villainous face"
  to survive an organisation chart.
- **E2 · Vesper alone, and the Pursuer's employer stays unnamed.** *Cost:*
  `World/History.md:13` continues to assert an institution no document describes;
  Vesper remains unlicensed at thirteen, which `Open Questions.md:11` is
  explicitly uneasy about; and `Themes and Motifs.md:55`'s contrast gives one
  half of itself to an ownerless process.
- **E3 · Split it deliberately.** The asylum predates Vesper and is not the
  state's — a charitable or private institution he came to run — while
  correction is state-scale because it rides the Ministry's loop. *Cost:* this is
  a coherent and interesting position ("the state with him as the engine but not
  as its author") but it needs one sentence in a document that owns "who may
  operate infrastructure," and no document currently does.

*Open question for the author:* is the asylum's origin the *same* fact as the
apparatus's operator, or two facts to be kept apart? Arc 2's pitfall
(`Story Plan/Choice Points.md:43`), Arc 2 scene 10's referral, F-016, F-022, and
E-001's closed sibling all key on the answer.

*Status impact:* E1 or E3 changes `World/History.md` (`:13`, and `:37`/`:39` are
the constraints it must respect or revise) and would close
`Open Questions.md:7` and `:11`.

*Note on a name.* If E1 is chosen, the authority needs a form of reference, and
naming it is the author's call — introducing a synonym for "the Ministry" or "the
state" would be a new finding, not a fix (F-006's territory).

## Cluster F — The city has two engineered failures and the story never compares them

*Observation.*

- `Characters/Claire.md:37` — "Claire was one of the younger children in a large
  family of prestigious architects. At the public debut of the family's grand
  building, the Static stopped and left her certain that something was wrong. She
  warned them without proof and with little tact; they dismissed her. The building
  later collapsed from an overlooked flaw and killed hundreds. To protect the
  family name, they blamed Claire."
- `World/Locations.md:15` — "It survives through excess, engineering, and a water
  system that appears improbably sustainable."
- `World/Locations.md:25` — "When the correction apparatus and water loop break,
  the eruption and clean rain are physical consequences rather than the universe
  approving Rain's choice."
- `Story Plan/1 The Spine.md:123` — "The clean rain follows the physical collapse
  of the apparatus and water loop."
- The terminology index records the collapse as "consistent (only statement)" —
  verified, and living only in Claire's sheet. Nothing in `World/` mentions it.

*Inference.* The city's identity is engineering that works; its record contains a
building that killed hundreds, and its ending contains a water system that
breaks. Both are failures of the same civic competence, one in a character's past
and one in the finale, and nothing relates them. A city's engineering culture is
one of the few places where "care stays distinct from substitution" and "evidence
stays distinct from identity" (`Story Plan/0 What is Remember Rain.md:15`) can be
made *physical* rather than argued: the building was judged by its record
after the fact, and Claire's entire discipline is modelling before.

**Options**

- **F1 · Leave them separate.** *Cost:* the city keeps a prestige-engineering
  identity with one disaster attached to a single character rather than to the
  city.
- **F2 · Relate at the level of institution, not event.** The architectural and
  water-engineering worlds are one small professional world, so the family's fall
  is a civic fact rather than only a private wound. *Cost:* must grant Claire no
  special insight into the loop. `Characters/Claire.md:55` requires her final act
  be "she says *Go* without a complete model," and `:91` — "Calculation never wins
  a fight by itself." A Claire who can see the caldera's flaw breaks both.
- **F3 · Relate at the level of record.** The collapse killed hundreds and the
  record was rewritten to protect a family (`Characters/Claire.md:37`); the
  state's other technology of accountability is extraction, which also removes
  the record of what happened (`World/Magic System.md:81`, `:91`). *Cost:* strong
  coupling, but it risks making Claire's backstory *illustrate* a theme rather
  than carry one — the specific failure this space is chartered to watch for.

*Open question for the author:* is the collapse a private wound Claire carries, or
a civic event the city carries — and does the story want the city to have any
memory of it at all?

## Cluster G — Roxana, fire, and the caldera: the option I recommend declining

*Observation.* Roxana is the one character whose element is the world's element.
`Characters/Roxana.md:41` — a childhood fire killed her family and scarred her
arms while she tried to save both parents. `:81` — Aster's lighter produces "one
small real resonant flame," with "negligible kinetic force" at base.
`World/Locations.md:23` — "The volcano anchors the city's water system."
`Story Plan/1 The Spine.md:123` — "Roxana burns Aster's shard and fills the
lighter with her own flame. The clean rain follows the physical collapse of the
apparatus and water loop."

*Inference.* The available move is to give her flame a relation to the volcanic
system — the finale already happens at the caldera, and the payoff would be
immediate.

*Recommendation: decline, on the documents' own evidence, not on taste.*

- `Characters/Roxana.md:111` — "Her Arc 5 victory is renewed choice, not greater
  stamina or uncomplicated strength."
- `Characters/The Pursuer.md:107` — "Roxana does not overpower his endurance; he
  empties while she repeatedly chooses to return."
- D-004 ruled that the Arc 3 spark is "**Roxana's own breakout**" — the ruling

- `World/Magic System.md:153` and `Story Plan/0 What is Remember Rain.md:13` — no
  immunity, no protagonist exception, no choice that can guarantee what it will
  make of you.

A resonance between her flame and the caldera converts repeated choosing into an
innate property, which is the one thing four documents agree her arc is not.

**Options**

- **G1 · Decline (recommended).** The flame's origin stays Aster's lighter plus
  her own scarred nervous system; the caldera stays impersonal. *Cost:* none. The
  story keeps Roxana's agency as the mechanism.
- **G2 · If any relation is wanted, put it on the cost side only.** Her scars and
  Empathic Burn make the wet, hot, crowded caldera a *worse* place for her than

  "Starborne carries rather than heals."
- **G3 · Full resonance.** *Cost:* contradicts `Roxana.md:111`, `The Pursuer.md:107`,
  D-004, and `Magic System.md:153` at once.

*Open question for the author:* does the finale want Roxana's body to be worse at
the caldera rather than better? That is the only version of this connection the
story's own rubric can carry.

## Cluster H — The recording layer is on the page and in no World document

*Observation.*

- `Characters/Shiori.md:81` — "Her first major project was an external recording
  system built after her injury. The state confiscated and scaled it into
  unattended surveillance infrastructure."
- `Story Plan/Themes and Motifs.md:129` — "Her recordings preserve evidence while
  the state ignores it."
- `:135` — "Keep the unattended surveillance system mostly invisible: no camera
  walls, checkpoints, or central tower."
- `Characters/Shiori.md:75` — "ownership, surveillance, and the cost of keeping
  the group connected remain her problems rather than errands for Rain."
- `Story Plan/1 The Spine.md:123` — "she turns the cameras off"
- The terminology index records Shiori's distributed rig as
  "`World/Locations.md` (not covered)."
- The newest prose puts it on the page: `Manuscript/Arc 1 - Fugitive/2 The
  vault.md:159` "SECURITY RECORDINGS WILL BE REVIEWED." · `:207` "A camera stared
  down at me above the doorway." · `:237` "Maybe the cameras were part of it too."

*Inference.* The recording layer is the only world system that touches all three
motif families at once — Record/Evidence/Trace (`Themes and Motifs.md:31`),
Contact/Intervention (whether watching is care or control, `:33`), and Arc 1
scene 3's accusation mechanism. It is now established on the page by the newest
prose and it is owned by no World document.

*Overlap, noted not re-raised:* E-019 already covers the Arc 5 cameras-off beat's
status conflict; E-017 covers the feeds as Arc 5's viewport; and F-011's candidate
resolutions already float "Gadget X is a recording or surveillance device" and
"Gadget X is a prototype of the correction apparatus." The *beat* is known. What
is uncovered is the *system* as a world fact.

**Options**

- **H1 · Give it a World entry, in `History` rather than `Locations`** (it is an
  institutional system, not a place): who operates it, that it descends from
  Shiori's confiscated recorder, and what it covers. *Cost:* `World/History.md:41`
  pushes mechanics and arcs out, and `Themes and Motifs.md:135` constrains it to
  stay invisible — so the entry must be described as a system with a scope, never
  as set-dressing.
- **H2 · Keep it unattributed.** *Cost:* the finale switches off something no
  document owns, and Rain's post-finale status
  (`Story Plan/0 What is Remember Rain.md:33`, `Open Questions.md:79`) has no
  stated mechanism either way.
- **H3 · Make recording the state's *detection* mechanism** — Deviants are found
  by being recorded, not by water. *Cost:* a significant re-reading of
  `World/Magic System.md:21` ("The state turned an uneven physical phenomenon into
  a class of people called Deviants") and `World/History.md:37`; but it would make
  Arc 1's accusation and Arc 4's exoneration the same mechanic, which may be the
  integration wanted, or too neat.

*Open question for the author:* is the surveillance system how the state *finds*
Deviants, or only how it *keeps* them? The difference decides whether the camera
above the vault door in Arc 1 is an accident or the whole plot in miniature.
