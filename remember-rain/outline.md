# Remember Rain — refined outline

> **Status: provisional (working).** Condensed from `Story Plan/1 The Spine.md`.
> The focus pass — inherited/underexplained mechanics · unfocused detail · vague/gesture prose — is **pending**.
> This lives outside the Scrivener export; accepted changes are pasted back into Scrivener by the author.

## Story hierarchy
- **Becoming is unavoidable.** Nobody can remain a perfectly preserved possibility.
- No choice can guarantee what it will make of you.
- Commitment ≠ momentum · participation ≠ control · care ≠ substitution · evidence ≠ identity · continuing ≠ re-choosing.
- Consequence is morally neutral.

## Genre & duration
Quest-primary, Horror-modulated. Route: commute → outbound train. ~5–8 weeks.

---

### Arc 1 · The Fugitive
**Choice pressure:** hiding can close into another commute, or follow the Ghost before he's ready.
- 0 Rain intro — dreams, self-awareness; Ghost diverts him from the commute (locked opening line).
- 1 The road less traveled — routine vs the Ghost's alley; he says *fuck it* and follows.
- 2 The vault — Shiori breaches Nimbus's vault for Gadget X; Rain follows, steals the goggles.
- 3 The charge lands — guilty of the goggles; falsely accused of Gadget X and the disappearances.
- 4 The drift — *pitfall entry.* Hiding becomes a workable routine.
- 5 The follow — a disappearance site full of Ministry personnel; no forced door.
- 6 Claire and Roxana save Rain — they were already investigating; Rain breaks Claire's model.
- 7 The hideout — first still group scene.
- 8 The trail — Claire traces the disappearances to the asylum.

### Arc 2 · The Mirror
**Choice pressure:** Vesper offers relief from wanting; Rain's *not yet* keeps the route moving.
- 1 The open door — the asylum is calm, useful, voluntary.
- 2 The demonstration — show Vesper's kindness (not assert it).
- 3 The Gamer and the Widower — enclosure; the first extraction that helped, and the returns.
- 4 The Fearless One — removed fear looks like the answer Rain wanted.
- 5 The Ruined One — committed once, ruined, and at peace with the chance being over.
- 6 The archive — *pitfall entry.* Vesper shows the shards; Rain nearly agrees, says *not yet*.
- 7 Aster's trail ends here — Vesper reacts to information he was never given; Roxana learns Aster's shard exists.
- 8 The Melt — Vesper extracts and Melts a memory-network tied to Roxana's pain; Rain charges three feet short.
- 9 The still room — Claire offers a path; Rain can't answer in the quiet; she gives the procedural word.
- 10 The referral — the Pursuer arrives and wins without raising a hand.

### Arc 3 · The Prisoner
**Choice pressure:** stay inside a possible life, or end even the beloved life; the Ghost goes to Roxana.
- 1 Intake — goggles, notebook, lighter logged into a tray; the tank, not a guard, is the lock.
- 2 True silence — sensory deprivation; Rain meets the suit-self that calls him a coward.
- 3 The lives — possible lives lived whole, then compressed; they feel real but can't acquire consequences.
- A life 1–3 — optional, self-contained lives.
- 4 The mountain — the beloved life; Rain ends it because a preserved possibility can't acquire consequences.
- 5 The hand — the only external POV cut; Roxana accepts the Ghost's open hand.
- 6 The carry — they recover the Imprints; Rain carries Roxana out of Aster's grave.

### Arc 4 · The Witness
**Choice pressure:** the Circuit Line gives Rain time to answer while the amnesty is live.
- 1 Out of the chute — escape into a smoother city; recover goggles and lighter.
- 2 The boundary — correction becomes measurable; Rain thins in corrected districts.
- 3 The accuser — Shiori's own recordings exonerate him; she stays.
- 4 The reveal — Roxana tells the truth about Vesper and the prison.
- 5 The advocate — Claire makes Vesper's case honestly.
- 6 The voice — Rain carries the Ruined One's case into his answer; Claire leaves shaken.
- 7 The amnesty — *pitfall entry.* The Pursuer's genuine exchange.
- 8 The unsigned offer — Rain refuses while the offer is live; his parents' silence was amputation.
- 9 The last stop — Perfect Clone manifests; Rain mistakes the projection for relief.

### Arc 5 · The Proof
**Convergence:** no pitfall. Shiori's network, Claire's refusal and overwatch, and Roxana's rearguard clear the path.
- 1 The summit road — the group climbs toward the caldera.
- 2 The room with Claire in it — she refuses extraction without Rain witnessing it.
- 3 The rearguard — Roxana holds the last gate; the Pursuer empties and stops walking.
- 4 The overwatch — Claire's model collapses; at contact range she says *Go*.
- 5 The extraction — direct contact succeeds; Vesper takes 100% of the target. Do not soften it.
- 6 The void — only *you* remains; the story survives as accurate fact without autobiographical access.
- 7 The return — Rain returns capable of responding, not newly completed; he catches Vesper himself.
- 8 The refusal — Rain refuses to remove the Split; breaks the correction apparatus.
- 9 The clean rain — brief aftermath (Shiori beat working, not locked); Roxana burns Aster's shard.
- 10 The outbound train — final scene; the notebook has two lines and many blank pages.
