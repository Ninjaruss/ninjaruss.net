---
title: "A Gap in Hue"
emblem: "/images/emblems/scroll.svg"
publishedAt: 2026-11-28
---
I wandered in an open field filled with costumes
Each with its own distinct colors
A mural in perfect harmony

I thought to reach out for once
But the thought greyed the scenery
Into the color of contrast

There I noticed a gap in hue
Wondering if color was there to be observed
Or a palette to draw from

I could not choose, yet the gap pained me
I stretched, I pushed, I moved my color
Only to see the same greyed scenery

But as I stared into the gap once more
My own hue had subtly shifted

### Written on November 28, 2026, after attending the Kitakyushu Pop Culture Festival alone.
