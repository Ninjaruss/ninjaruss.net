## The Pursuer

A man who has served the same institution for thirty years and calculated the date he runs empty.

### Quick Read

- **Age:** roughly 50–55
- **Height:** 6'2"
- **Role:** institutional pursuer, practical combat threat, and Rain's strongest foil
- **Contradiction:** nothing can stop him, and he wants nothing.
- **Question:** *Is it too late to stop?*

His career predates the Flare; his supernatural condition does not. Thirty years of service supplied the heading, and the last five years turned endurance and wanting into fuel. He is a person who knows the cost, the end date, and the possibility of stopping, not a faceless force that mistakes obedience for innocence.

### On the Page

**Around fifty to fifty-five.** Main trait: **Procedural Persistence** — courteous, unhurried continuity supplied by the institution's next verb. Passive trait: **Buried Longing** — the enormous want he never voiced, burned as fuel for somebody else's direction. The longing rarely redirects the procedure; it escapes as steam and surfaces once as the possibility that stopping need not erase the man who endured.

He knows exactly what has happened to him. His tragedy is not ignorance but the fact that he has spent all his endurance continuing and none of it leaving.

### Want, Fear, and Change

The Pursuer mistakes endurance for identity. Stopping appears to invalidate the person who spent thirty years continuing.

He once wanted to find people moving toward the same thing with equal force. He never asked for that life. The institution supplied a direction instead, and eventually the borrowed heading became the only self he could recognize.

1. Steam and a distant figure give the hunt a human shape.
2. In round one, he loses only the exit and does not chase beyond the task.
3. In round two, paperwork and procedure defeat the group without a raised hand.
4. His signature appears on Aster's intake record.
5. He presents a genuine amnesty and reads his own disposal date without expression. Rain asks whether it is too late to stop; the steam reacts once.
6. Against Roxana, he spends ahead of schedule, empties, and stops walking. Steam condenses on cold wet wool.

He does not complete a conversion or choose a new side. His final change is the terminal fact that the old direction can no longer carry him.

### With Others

- **Rain:** Rain wants without acting. The Pursuer acts without wanting. Rain fears losing possible futures; the Pursuer fears betraying the accumulated past.
- **Vesper:** the Pursuer surrenders choice to process. Vesper claims choice over everyone else. One obeys the institution's verb; the other cannot wait for institutions or people.
- **Roxana:** he can continue one old decision until the fuel ends. She can stop, hurt, and choose to relight. She does not overpower him; her repeated returns force him to keep paying for the assigned result.
- **Aster:** the Pursuer's signature on Aster's intake record makes him part of the history Roxana inherits. The record matters more than a confession, apology, or explanatory flashback.

### Burning, Greatcoat, and Finite Fuel

Shared passive, Imprint, Deviation, and cost rules belong to `World/Magic System.md`. For the Pursuer on the page:

- **Burning / furnace:** the wanting he once carried becomes internal heat and inexhaustible-looking motion.
- **The greatcoat:** decades-old field wool that became his resonant Imprint after the Flare. It traps and shapes vapor into a boundary layer that turns blades and flash-boils rain.
- **Armor:** the defense is steam, not plate. Plate armor is the city's myth of him.
- **Cost:** every defense and completed task burns more of what remains. He wins exchanges by emptying himself. Running empty means he finally stops walking.

His combat presence is practical: he advances, takes the shortest sufficient action, uses vapor to control contact and defense, and refuses to spend effort beyond the task. He feels inevitable because he does not hurry, not because he is tactically omnipotent.

### Visual Anchors

The Pursuer has the cast's largest and heaviest-feeling silhouette. The greatcoat, steam, upright posture, deliberate walking, and wet wool at the end are essential. He should feel inevitable rather than aggressive. Avoid exaggerated bodybuilder proportions, literal plate armor, or sudden bursts of speed.

### Do Not Flatten

- He never runs and never lies.
- Courtesy, restraint, accurate paperwork, and the old unasked desire keep him human without excusing what he does.
- Do not reduce him to a symbolic obstacle. His procedural decisions should have concrete tactical and institutional consequences.
- He never allies, supplies intelligence, joins the heroes, or receives a redemption scene. The gate simply stops being manned.
- The coat does not come off as a cure.
- Roxana does not overpower his endurance; he empties while she repeatedly chooses to return.
- Nobody later identifies the steam's reaction to Rain's question.
- His final stop is unrecorded. Nobody thanks him, and he files nothing.
- Endurance is not the answer. Endurance without re-choosing is momentum.
