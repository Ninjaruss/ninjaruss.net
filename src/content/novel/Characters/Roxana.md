## Roxana

She can give almost anything and cannot receive without feeling exposed.

### Quick Read

- **Age:** 27–28
- **Height:** 5'5"
- **Role:** fighter, caretaker, and Rain's first home after the charge
- **Contradiction:** she can give anything and cannot receive.
- **Question:** *Can I trust my worth when nobody needs what I provide?*

Roxana uses usefulness as evidence that she belongs. Her care is sincere; its force also keeps other people from seeing what she wants. She takes responsibility for the immediate body, hunger, wound, or threat in front of her, then turns that response into protection, work, and physical action.

### On the Page

- Warm, direct, tactile, and physically immediate.
- Feeds people and decides quickly that they are hers to protect.
- Touches people while speaking; the other main characters generally do not.
- Hands someone a task instead of answering a question about herself.
- Acts on visible pain before a full explanation is available.
- Relentless rather than serene. Her warmth has pressure behind it.
- Anger enters through the body: a set stance, a hand already moving, a barrier or opponent met directly.

She takes Rain in before asking about the accusation. She also trains him toward Aster without admitting that need. Neither fact cancels the other.

### Want, Fear, and Change

A childhood fire killed Roxana's family and scarred her arms while she tried to save both parents. Aster later became her father figure and trainer. After he disappeared, she took his surname as a vow and kept his empty lighter.

Roxana wants to be needed and known, but believes nobody leans on someone whose limits they have seen. She makes herself indispensable so belonging never has to survive her replaceability. Strength is not ease for her; it is the responsibility to keep returning when protection still matters.

The sharper test is not whether she can accept a hand once. It is whether she can remain in the room while someone else cooks, protects, decides, or carries and still believe she belongs.

1. She appears at full fighting capability and wins an exit from the Pursuer.
2. She accepts Vesper's steadying hand and loses the memory-network that lets her stand as she did before.
3. In the prison, time without anyone needing her wears through the armor of usefulness.
4. She accepts the Ghost's open hand and produces a raw spark without the lighter.
5. Rain carries her out. Arc 4 requires her to accept what was done rather than letting the rescue settle the question for her.
6. She tells the truth flatly over an ordinary task.
7. Against the Pursuer, she loses exchanges and repeatedly chooses to re-enter until he empties.
8. She burns Aster's shard and fills his lighter with her own flame.

### With Others

- **Claire:** Roxana meets the visible wound first; Claire needs to understand it. Each approach is necessary and each can become avoidance. Roxana is not intuition positioned against Claire's reason.
- **Vesper:** both intervene through real care. Roxana spends herself while preserving the other person's agency. Vesper spends part of the other person to preserve the successful result.
- **Rain:** she offers home, training, and direction. She also tries to rebuild Aster through him without explaining that motive.
- **The Pursuer:** he can continue one old decision until the fuel ends. She can stop, hurt, and choose to relight. Their last fight turns repeated choice, not superior force, into the difference.
- **Aster:** father figure, trainer, missing investigator, and the source of her surname and lighter. His absence remains active through what she does with his training and remains.

### Empathic Burn and Bearing Flame

Shared passive, Imprint, Deviation, and cost rules belong to `World/Magic System.md`. For Roxana on the page:

- **Empathic burn:** other people's pain reaches her through the burn scars on her arms. She cannot choose whether to feel it.
- **Bearing Flame:** accessed through Aster's empty lighter. Fire holds up a body that cannot otherwise stand; it does not restore that body.
- **Cost:** the flame uses Roxana's own body heat. Pain arrives uninvited, but offered warmth enters only when she accepts it. When the flame gutters, she becomes completely dependent on others.
- **Passive breakout:** in the prison, accepting the Ghost's open hand lets her produce one raw spark without the lighter. It nearly empties her and is not a new reliable technique.

Vesper Melts a memory-network tied to her trauma, pain, and refusal to stop. Roxana loses access to it permanently. He gains no autobiographical memory, but may inherit unyielding reflexes or behaviors without their meaning.

### Visual Anchors

Roxana is compact and physically forceful. Burn scars on her arms are essential. Her warmth should come through posture, touch, practical hands, anger, and workwear rather than delicacy. The chair and lighter are key objects. When the Bearing Flame stands her, movement remains costly rather than becoming a graceful restoration fantasy.

### Still Flexible

- Whether the childhood fire created her need to provide or became a debt she uses to justify a need that was already present.

### Do Not Flatten

- Do not reduce her to a woman waiting to be retrieved.
- Her inability to stand is produced by Vesper's memory alteration, not a spinal injury.
- The scars are the channel: pain arrives through them; offered warmth must be accepted.
- Her giving is both love and armor. Do not reveal one as fake.
- Training Rain is partly an attempt to reconstitute Aster. Never have her explain this.
- Her anger and immediate action are practical intelligence, not evidence that she is less rational than Claire.
- Her Arc 5 victory is renewed choice, not greater stamina or uncomplicated strength.
- Receiving care does not cure her or make dependence shameful.
- Avoid frail-tragic-woman imagery and the serene disabled-sage archetype.
