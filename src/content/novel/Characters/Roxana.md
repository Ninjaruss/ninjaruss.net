\#\# Roxana

She keeps trying despite her limits, but fears that needing help will cost her acceptance.

\#\#\# Quick Read

\- \*\*Age:\*\* 27–28

\- \*\*Role:\*\* fighter, caretaker, and Rain's first home after the charge

\- \*\*Traits:\*\* \*\*Forceful Warmth\*\* / \*\*Guarded Neediness\*\*

\- \*\*Contradiction:\*\* her self-reliance is real, but she feels she must prove she can manage alone to be accepted.

\- \*\*Question:\*\* \*Can I believe people will still want me around when they see what I cannot manage alone?\*

Roxana believes people accept her because she is capable on her own. Her resourcefulness and persistence are genuine strengths she values; she can enjoy solving a problem, adapting to a limit, and making another attempt. The pressure comes when managing alone becomes something she must demonstrate to remain welcome. Usefulness is one way she establishes that security. Her care is sincere: she responds to the immediate body, hunger, wound, or threat in front of her through protection, work, and physical action.

\#\#\# On the Page

\- Warm, direct, tactile, and physically immediate.

\- Feeds people and decides quickly that they are hers to protect.

\- Touches people while speaking; the other main characters generally do not.

\- Hands someone a task instead of answering a question about herself.

\- Acts on visible pain before a full explanation is available.

\- Relentless rather than serene. Her warmth has pressure behind it.

\- Anger enters through the body: a set stance, a hand already moving, a barrier or opponent met directly.

She takes Rain in before asking about the accusation. Welcoming and training him can be pleasurable acts of care as well as ways to feel secure in what she brings to a relationship. Do not make every generous or competent act a bid for acceptance.

\#\#\# Want, Fear, and Change

Roxana's burns are a persistent condition rather than a single event's scar: not solved, not solvable, managed daily with small accommodations and accepted as a fact of her life so she can stop spending her life on solving it. \{TK origin: what the burns are and where they came from — author's call, deliberately not decided here\}

What she learned from it is the loneliness: people who hear about the pain and state the obvious. \*Have you tried not doing that. Have you tried this product.\* Advice that is not unhelpful so much as tone-deaf, from people who never considered she had already tried it. Such encounters make offers of help difficult to trust, especially when they overlook the work she already does to manage. Her response need not be the same with every person or kind of assistance; relief and exposure can coexist. She carries a lighter that does not work.

Roxana wants to be needed and known, yet fears that visible dependence will change whether people want her around. Providing something valuable does not necessarily reassure her if she still needs help herself. She can mistake an assisted achievement for evidence that her own capability was insufficient. Strength is not ease for her; she can keep trying through difficulty without being able to guarantee that she can carry it alone.

Accepting one hand does not settle this belief. Vesper's intervention gives her a real harmful experience to contend with, not merely an unfounded reluctance others can talk away. Her change includes remaining in the room while someone else cooks, protects, decides, or carries and believing she is still wanted. Receiving care can coexist with agency, persistence, and renewed capability; later strength must not prove that she never needed anyone. She can still feel exposed or return to proving herself afterward.

1\. She appears at full fighting capability and wins an exit from the Pursuer.

2\. She accepts Vesper's steadying hand and loses the memory-network that lets her stand as she did before.

3\. In the prison, time without anyone needing her removes a familiar way to feel secure, while her own need challenges the belief that acceptance depends on managing alone.

4\. She accepts the Ghost's open hand and produces a raw spark without the lighter.

5\. Rain carries her out. Arc 4 requires her to accept what was done rather than letting the rescue settle the question for her.

6\. She tells the truth flatly over an ordinary task.

7\. Against the Pursuer, she loses exchanges and repeatedly chooses to re-enter until he empties.

8\. She remains in the room while someone else cooks, protects, decides, and carries — and still believes she belongs. She sits down while someone else works.

\#\#\# With Others

\- \*\*Claire:\*\* Roxana meets the visible wound first; Claire needs to understand it. Each approach is necessary and each can become avoidance. Roxana is not intuition positioned against Claire's reason.

\- \*\*Vesper:\*\* both intervene through real care. Roxana spends herself while preserving the other person's agency. Vesper spends part of the other person to preserve the successful result.

\- \*\*Rain:\*\* she offers home, training, and direction. He is also the first person in a long time who needed her without first telling her how to manage herself.

\- \*\*The Pursuer:\*\* he can continue one old decision until the fuel ends. She can stop, hurt, and choose to relight. Their last fight turns repeated choice, not superior force, into the difference.

\#\#\# Empathic Burn, Undying Flame, and Starborne

Shared passive, Imprint, Deviation, and cost rules belong to \`World/Magic System.md\`. For Roxana on the page:

\- \*\*Empathic Burn:\*\* Roxana involuntarily echoes nearby bodily pain through her own nervous system. It is not emotional mind-reading. Sensitive or damaged tissue responds most strongly, so her scars amplify the experience without serving as its exclusive channel. Exact range and source localization remain unsettled. The faculty is not itself suppressed by a deprivation pod; it goes quiet because everyone near her is suppressed, leaving nothing to echo. It registers only what exceeds that.

\- \*\*Undying Flame:\*\* her own empty lighter produces one small real resonant flame guided primarily through Roxana's gestures. Its core resists ordinary rain, wind, and smothering while sustained, and it can ignite ordinary combustibles; secondary fires remain ordinary and uncontrolled. The base flame has negligible kinetic force.

\- \*\*Base cost:\*\* sustaining the flame heightens Roxana's nociceptive sensitivity. Her injuries, scar pain, and incoming Empathic Burn become harder to ignore and can interfere with function.

\- \*\*Starborne:\*\* the advanced expression binds Roxana's movement to the same tiny flame. Guiding it can pull, lift, pivot, redirect, and carry her body along its path. It does not heal her or restore function Vesper removed; inertia, impact, injury, and structural limits remain.

\- \*\*Starborne cost:\*\* normal pain gating is suppressed; ungated pain can trigger withdrawal, tension, and collapse (see \`World/Magic System.md\`).

\- \*\*Passive breakout:\*\* in the prison, Empathic Burn carries the change out of Rain's pod and she knows she is not alone — and, sealed in, cannot reach him. Accepting the Ghost's open hand lets her produce one raw spark without the lighter. It nearly empties her and is not a new reliable technique.

Undying Flame and Starborne are alternate deliberate modes, not independent powers used together. The star never gets bigger; Roxana changes her relationship to it.

Vesper Melts the decisions that made her keep standing — the accumulated choices to re-enter, to carry, to not stop. Roxana loses access to them permanently. He gains no lived memory, but may inherit unyielding reflexes or behaviors without their meaning.

\#\#\# Visual Anchors

Roxana is compact and physically forceful. Burn scars on her arms are essential. Her warmth should come through posture, touch, practical hands, anger, and workwear rather than delicacy. The chair, lighter, and one small star-like flame are key images. When Starborne carries her, movement remains costly rather than becoming a graceful restoration fantasy.

\#\#\# Do Not Flatten

\- Do not reduce her to a woman waiting to be retrieved.

\- Her inability to stand is produced by Vesper's memory alteration, not a spinal injury.

\- The scars amplify bodily pain; they are not the sole channel and Empathic Burn is not emotional mind-reading.

\- Her giving is both love and armor. Do not reveal one as fake.

\- Her burns are a managed ongoing condition, not a tragic wound. Avoid the single-catastrophe origin and the heroic scar.

\- Offered help that lands as dismissal is her real antagonist. The Ghost's hand works in Arc 3 because it asks nothing and says nothing.

\- Her anger and immediate action are practical intelligence, not evidence that she is less rational than Claire.

\- Her Arc 5 victory is renewed choice, not greater stamina or uncomplicated strength.

\- Starborne carries rather than heals. Do not let the flame restore removed function or erase injury and inertia.

\- Receiving care does not cure her or make dependence shameful. Preserve the satisfaction in her real capability without making solitary endurance the price of belonging.

\- Avoid frail-tragic-woman imagery and the serene disabled-sage archetype.

\#\#\# Working Questions — Not Settled

\- Which kinds of assistance feel most exposing, and with whom? Practical help, information, physical support, and emotional accompaniment need not mean the same thing to her. No new origin or history is established by this question.

\- Does she sincerely welcome needs in others that she believes would make her own presence unacceptable? This unequal standard is a possibility to explore, not a settled rule for every relationship.
