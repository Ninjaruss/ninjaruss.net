## Shiori

A tinkerer who made one very good thing, lost it to the state, and kept making imperfect things anyway.

### Quick Read

- **Age:** 25–26
- **Height:** 5'8"
- **Role:** maker, recorder, accuser, and the fourth front of the finale
- **Traits:** **Playful Industriousness** / **Defensive Vigilance**
- **Contradiction:** she cannot reliably hold her own life and is the most present person in any room.
- **Question:** *If I cannot preserve the thing itself, can I trust what it changed?*

Shiori asked Vesper for help once and was damaged by an early, imprecise extraction. She never remembers making that request. Since then, she has trusted machines rather than people to hold her place.

Stress worsens her memory condition; calm routines help stabilize it. Narration, deployed tools, backups, and repeated work habits are accommodations rather than decorative eccentricities.

### On the Page

- Fast, cheerful, kinetic, and always using her hands.
- Narrates her work aloud as a memory aid disguised as chatter.
- Leaves each tool on the exact seam where she stopped. The tool is a bookmark, not clutter.
- Marks her work with a doodle, charm, or boot-screen joke, never a formal logo.
- Builds redundancy into small objects because she is bracing for the next loss.
- Occasionally freezes to recover her place, then continues without self-pity.

Her maker's marks are playful. Her survival system is not. Keep that gap visible.

### Want, Fear, and Change

Shiori is not trying to recover a lost past. She is defending a future self against another disappearance, while building also lets her avoid asking what she actually needs. She will not ask a person to remember for her.

She wants accompaniment without having to earn it through usefulness, though she would not admit that. The current Arc 5 action is deliberately small: she closes something without marking her place. Its thematic interpretation remains a working direction rather than locked canon.

1. She appears unnamed in the vault, taking back confiscated work, and remembers Rain's nervous laugh.
2. Her equipment and maker's marks recur before she joins the cast.
3. She boards the Circuit Line to accuse Rain. Her own recordings exonerate him, and she stays.
4. She carries the knowledge that the system saving them is also the system watching the city.
5. She runs the finale's fourth front and spends the rig that has served as an archive of herself.
6. **Working beat, not locked:** she turns the cameras off, puts a tool away instead of leaving it as a bookmark, and keeps Rain's laugh without notes.

The story never treats her original request as wrong. She asked the one person for whom asking was dangerous.

### Working Direction — Continuity Without Completion

**This section is a developed proposal, not hard canon.** Preserve the established facts above even if this interpretation changes.

The proposed formulation is that Shiori is the cast member most comfortable with something being unfinished and least comfortable with it being unrecorded. Evidence is not a verdict or authority for her; it is a bookmark that lets her re-enter an unfinished process.

- **Rain / Shiori:** Rain treats evidence as a verdict and avoids producing it. Shiori treats evidence as a bookmark and uses it to return. His movement is toward creating evidence without needing it to prove him; her proposed movement is toward letting one moment matter without guaranteeing a receipt. Her activity is not his cure.
- **Vesper / Shiori:** Vesper treats completed evidence as authority, corrects the source, and removes the seam. Shiori patches the particular thing and lets the seam show that something happened there. Both build systems against loss, and neither is a simple healthy answer.
- **Claire / Shiori:** Claire models forward by reducing variables; Shiori bookmarks backward by preserving enough context to re-enter. Both build systems against future loss, one through prediction and one through redundancy.
- **Roxana / Shiori:** Roxana fears replaceability; Shiori fears erasure. Both provide rather than ask directly for accompaniment.
- **Pursuer / Shiori:** the Pursuer's record turns past continuity into an obligation to keep moving. Shiori's record may provide enough context to re-enter and choose again: momentum versus re-entry.

The proposed Arc 5 “no bookmark” wager must not become a cure. She may forget the moment. The possibility being tested is that it can still have mattered without a guaranteed receipt, not that she now trusts her memory or stops using accommodations.

Shiori enters with pressure independent of the central conflict: her work was stolen, Rain appears to have taken it, and the state has turned her survival system into infrastructure. Even after her accusation fails, ownership, surveillance, and the cost of keeping the group connected remain her problems rather than errands for Rain.

### Work, Tools, Records, and Cost

Shiori has no passive, bonded Imprint, or Deviation of her own. That absence is not a measure of human capacity or worth.

- Her first major project was an external recording system built after her injury. The state confiscated and scaled it into unattended surveillance infrastructure. The same system later preserves evidence everyone else ignored.
- The goggles are her work. Rain wears her decisions from Arc 1 onward before knowing who made them.
- Her recorder and surviving records first support her accusation, then correct it. Evidence can outlast her certainty without becoming neutral or institutionally innocent.
- Most of her devices are handmade, patched, and specific to a problem. Functional redundancy and playful maker's marks have different jobs and should not blur together.
- In Arc 5 she burns out her own rig component by component to maintain feeds across every front. This is a chosen material cost, not a surprise failure.

### Visual Anchors

Shiori is wiry, kinetic, hands-busy, and slightly improvised. Tools, functional patches, handmade modifications, a recorder, the goggles, and small maker traces matter more than exact fashion. She should look like someone who occupies space by doing things in it. Avoid decorative “quirky engineer” clutter with no functional reason.

### Do Not Flatten

- She is never cured and does not need a refusal scene.
- Learning what Vesper did must affect her; continuing does not mean she is untouched.
- Do not make her connective tissue for Rain, Claire, and Roxana. Her accusation, stolen work, surveillance complicity, memory condition, and spent rig exert independent pressure.
- She never states the theme or explains what memory means.
- Her goggles and recorder are made objects with compromises, not neutral gear.
- Keep one quiet beat of complicity about the surveillance system. Do not turn it into a guilt speech.
- Her connection with Rain remains an allusion, not a reward, cure, confession, kiss, or genre-signaled romance.
- In Arc 5 she does not reassure Rain that his memories are his. She continues beside him without treating his condition as an emergency.
