Context: After the emotional struggle and self-deprecation from Rain occurs, each memory from the story is taken away from him. The screen is black and leaves us with only the player. The perspective shifts to 2nd person perspective and lets the player make the conscious decision to keep choosing. To either commit to being a full self (commitment) or a diminished self (safety). As the player continues to keep choosing to commit and do things with very little immediate reward, the ui and music slowly are built up again to symbolize Rain is being built back up.
