Rain back able to respond — not newly completed, and not a newfound self (Rain's "Do Not Flatten"). He reaches for the PERFECT CLONE, the move that has worked all arc, and Vesper is ready for it: he holds every decision Rain ever made. The counterfeit was always a bluff that force passes through. Vesper ignores it, reaches the real body, and Rain is badly hurt — his own relapse used against him by the man carrying the complete record of it.

Ends on THE SMILE. The asylum patient's smile from Arc 2 — "the one that angered me so much without any reasonable explanation why" (your drafted prose, Arc 5 · 5) — arriving now with everything still in it: the fear, the failure, the un-owned past, the Split he is about to refuse to lose. Vesper has produced that expression by subtraction many times and has never seen it arrive on its own. It is not confidence. He may still lose.
