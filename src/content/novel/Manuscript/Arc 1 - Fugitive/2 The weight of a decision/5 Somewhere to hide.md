{TK connect the previous card’s decision to leave with his existing search for somewhere to hide.}

Why can’t I bring myself to give this up? 
I am scurrying around the city to look for places to briefly hide. A tiny, but high-pitched chirp from my wrist brought my mind back to reality. How long will I have to do this for?
I wandered about the city. Funnily enough, I never really explored much of it. Always seemed like I would rather just get things done then head immediately home. 
I felt an odd… disconnect? As I walked through the crowds of people, I realized their gazes were like a set parameter programmed within them. What I mean was that everyone seemed to be in their own little world as their eyes seemed to look only for immediate problems or obstacles. 
Not people with a bounty on their head.
I should be feeling a sense of relief, yet the grip on my chest dragged. 
It feels wrong. I know it’s wrong. I am having trouble finding the words to describe it. Though, it’s probably cause I am in the wrong.

I approached what seemed to be a run-down mall. The shops seemed to be the sort where only locals maybe visited out of only mere convenience, or perhaps out of pity.
Either way, this place will have to do.
I placed my bag off to the side of a nearby post then stood off to the side to stare at the sky. The drizzle proceeded without end.
“Ack-!” A flash of light blinded my eyes for a split moment. I turned my head to see what the light was, but was only met with the normalcy of a run down set of buildings. There was no noise?
