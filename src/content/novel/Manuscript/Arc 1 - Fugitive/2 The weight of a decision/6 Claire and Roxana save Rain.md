I strolled my way in the direction of the flashed light. I wondered if it was an electrical malfunction or something?
As I approached the buildings, there was a small alleyway in between… or simply a dark hallway. It was extremely dark even though evening just hit a moment ago.
I heard a faint huffing. There was someone here. I walked further into the passageway, and was met with a bash to my face.
I tried to hurry up onto my feet to withdraw. A hard boot was firmly placed on my chest.
“Wait! Wait!” I yelled hoping the boot would not dig further deeper. My chest twisted out of place for lack of a better term. 
Almost instantaneously I felt the weight off my chest. The person before me wore a dark grey raincoat, except its sleeves were unusually tied up with belts. 
“You need to leave.” She said as she pointed towards the exit with her arm with a somewhat crooked, but stiff gesture. 
My strained chest awkwardly tensed as I made my intention to leave.
I simply stood there. I could not explain why. Perhaps my body was simply stunned from the previous impact, or perhaps I needed time to think about what just happened.
“Please… Leave. Now.” Her voice faltered for a split moment before becoming rigid. 
Even I could recognize the thinness of her breathe. 
“A-are you-u a-alright?” I asked as the words struggled to escape my lungs.
Her stance seemed to lighten for a split moment before tensing back.
“Leave.”
“Not yet. I don’t think I want to be out there…”
The rain was noticeably stronger than it was before.
“...”, She proceeded to hobble her way a short distance before resting her back on the wall. 
Likewise, I did the same.
She pulled out what seemed to be a metallic thin card and swayed it around while staring at it. 
It kind of felt like we were waiting for the next train, except I was simply here to avoid sitting out in the open.
After maybe five minutes, she slid the card back into her cloak.

The alleyway was blasted with bright light from the entrance. 
A set of brightly tinted grey soldiers appeared armed with rifles and military gear.
I glanced behind me to find the woman behind me retreat into her cloak. 
I held my hands up as an attempt to seem I was some innocent bystander.
