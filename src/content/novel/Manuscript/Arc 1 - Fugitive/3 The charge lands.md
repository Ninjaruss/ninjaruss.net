Well this certainly wasn’t part of any plan. I’d like to think I am prepared for the worst, but actually, that is a complete and utter lie.

“I’ll figure it out” is probably my default plan.

The wanted notice on the screen slowly clouded my mind.

They do not have a clear picture of my face. They have not arrived at my doorstep. They do not know I only stole one thing. They did not even notice \*she\* was there. 

They don’t know shit.

Now what am I supposed to do?

I reach for my phone to find anything that could help my current situation.

\*3 Missed Calls from Mom\*

How do I begin explaining this to her? That I might go to jail for stealing a bargain bin accessory?

I drop the phone onto the table.

A familiar tension spread from my chest to my fingertips. My muscles stiffened, as though my body were bracing for something.

I could already imagine trying to explain myself to them, only to walk away feeling misunderstood once again.

…

I have to try anyway.

…
