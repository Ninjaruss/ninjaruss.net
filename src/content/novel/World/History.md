### Timeline / History

*Added 2026-08-19. Years are relative to the Flare (Flare = Year 0), not calendar dates — consistent with how the rest of the plan tracks decisions by process date, not story date. Present day is **Year 55.** Anchors below make the ages and durations already written elsewhere (the Pursuer's thirty years, Vesper's mid-to-late forties, Shiori's injury reading as "early" in Vesper's career) actually consistent, not just plausible in isolation.*

- **Pre-Flare (before Year 0).** Ordinary world. No Deviations, no Imprints, no Water Ministry. The island city exists already, built into and around the dormant volcano, for reasons that predate anything supernatural — a port, a resource, an accident of geography. The Flare does not create the city. It creates everything the city becomes obsessed with.

- **Year 0 — the Flare.** Unseen energy sweeps the world in an instant, clinging only to those mid-commitment, and to objects that witnessed one. It infuses the volcano's water. Nobody understands it yet; there is no state apparatus, no sorting, no word for what happened.

- **Years 0–15 — the city rebuilds around the loop.** The Sacred Water's amplification is discovered, then chased. This is the closest thing this world has to a golden age: the loop is strong, the population is disproportionately in motion (rebuilding tends to produce commitment), and the city that exists now — Nimbus-built infrastructure, the Circuit Line, the vault architecture — is largely constructed in this window, for reasons that had nothing to do with control yet.

- **Years 15–30 — the sort calcifies.** As the initial burst of collective motion settles into ordinary life, fewer people generate new Deviations, and the state — already administering the water infrastructure — starts administering the *people* the water responds to. "Deviation" moves from a private, felt thing to a filed one. The Water Ministry's bureaucracy dates from here: forms, registries, the first pods. This is not yet Vesper's crime; it is the crime that makes his possible.

- **Year 3 — the Pursuer is born.** Recruited at **Year 25**, early twenties, already a furnace, already burning for something he never asked anyone for. Thirty years of service brings him to the story's present at Year 55.

- **Year 8 — Vesper is born.** His childhood runs through the sort calcifying — he grows up inside a bureaucracy that already treats wanting as something to be filed, which is the water he swims in before he ever founds anything of his own. The mother-wound (see Vesper's character doc) lands somewhere around **Year 17** (age 9), never dramatised, never dated more precisely than that on the page.

- **Years 30–45 — the loop visibly fades.** The population's collective motion thins as the state's management of "deviance" produces exactly the stagnation it was built to prevent. Weather thins with it.

- **Year 27 — Roxana is born.** The fire that takes her family and scars her arrives in her childhood, roughly **Year 35–37**. Aster becomes her father figure shortly after, trains her through her teens, and dies hunting Vesper — never confirmed to her — around **Year 48–50**, when she is in her early twenties. She takes his surname as an adult vow shortly after.

- **Year 40 — Vesper opens the asylum,** early and imprecise, in his early thirties. **Year 45 — Shiori's injury,** age 16, five years into his practice — early enough to read as a mistake, not yet a method.

- **Year 29 — Shiori is born.**

- **Years 40–55 — Vesper refines his technique** across roughly fifteen years of practice. The Widower's nine extractions (see The Widower's character file) cluster in the back half of this window, once the method is precise — function intact, self absent. Aster's death (Year 48–50) falls inside this same stretch: he was hunting a man who had, by then, been operating for close to a decade.

- **Year 33 — Claire is born.** The building collapse that ends her family's trust in her lands in her early-to-mid teens, roughly **Year 47–49**.

- **Year 32 — Rain is born.**

- **Year 55 — present.** The water is thin. The correction is rolling out. Rain steals the goggles.
