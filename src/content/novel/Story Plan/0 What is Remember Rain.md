## What is Remember Rain?

### Premise

*Remember Rain* is a visual novel about a young man who leaves his commute on impulse, steals a resonant object, and is blamed for disappearances caused by someone who can remove memory and wanting.

Rain has spent his life preserving dreams by never testing them. As he moves through an asylum, a sensory-deprivation prison, and a city accepting painless correction, he must act without receiving proof that action will make him exceptional or safe.

### Player Experience

The player helps Rain continue through one route while four comfortable exits offer lives in which the pressure to choose can stop. Commitment is expressed by continuing rather than selecting a heroic answer. Leaving a pitfall reconverges with the route so one attentive playthrough contains the complete plot.

### Story Promise

The story takes Rain's avoidance seriously without making action a guarantee of success. Care, certainty, endurance, relief, and remembered experience can all help a person act; each becomes dangerous when treated as sufficient proof of who that person is or what someone else should choose for them.

### Ending Intent

Vesper's extraction succeeds. Rain knows the completed adventure as accurate fact, but he does not feel that he literally lived it. Remembering does not complete him, certify his growth, or reconnect him to a finished self.

Rain also remains publicly a fugitive. He boards an outbound train because leaving is necessary and because he chooses a first destination, a new adventure, and the next step toward his dreams. The final choice matters without vindication, certainty, or recovered ownership of the past.
