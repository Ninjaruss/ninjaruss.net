A city that manages its own decline. A government that keeps the peace by keeping everyone comfortable. And Rain — a dreamer stuck in the waiting room of his own life — who breaks his commute one morning to follow a shadow, and becomes a fugitive for it.

*Remember Rain* is a visual novel about the cost of staying "full of potential": memory as the record of the self, comfort as a slow self-erasure, and the irreversible commitments that build a person. The spine is mostly linear on purpose — it branches only at the few choices that matter, because choice is the theme.

It's being written scene by scene, in the open. The desk below is the live workbench: script on paper, planning in ink.
