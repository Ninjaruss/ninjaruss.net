## What is Remember Rain?

### Premise

*Remember Rain* is a visual novel about a young man who leaves his commute on impulse, steals a resonant object, and is blamed for disappearances caused by someone who can remove memory and wanting.

Rain has spent his life preserving dreams by never testing them. As he moves through an asylum, a sensory-deprivation prison, and a city accepting painless correction, he must act without receiving proof that action will make him exceptional or safe.

### Story Hierarchy

**Becoming is unavoidable.** People are embodied, limited, conditioned, and continually changed by reality; nobody can remain a perfectly preserved possibility.

The human problem is how to live under uncertainty and consequence when no choice can guarantee what it will make of you. Rain's particular defense is to preserve possibility so that a finished attempt cannot become finite evidence of what he can do or who he is.

Participation and commitment allow choices to acquire consequences, but consequence is morally neutral. A commitment may help, harm, liberate, imprison, fail, or outlive its reason. The story therefore keeps commitment distinct from momentum, participation from control, care from substitution, evidence from identity, and continuing from re-choosing.

### Player Experience

The player helps Rain continue through one route while four comfortable exits offer lives in which the pressure to choose can stop. Commitment is expressed by continuing rather than selecting a heroic answer. Leaving a pitfall reconverges with the route so one attentive playthrough contains the complete plot.

### Genre Architecture

The story is **Quest-primary with strong Horror modulation**. The Quest supplies the route from commute to outbound train; Horror supplies the increasing pressure as correction makes the city easier, quieter, and less alive. Battle is localized: Roxana and the Pursuer form the strongest recurring Battle subplot, while other fights become Battle-shaped only when overcoming the opponent is the immediate dramatic question.

### Story Promise

The story takes Rain's avoidance seriously without making action a guarantee of success. Care, certainty, endurance, relief, and remembered experience can all help a person act; each becomes dangerous when treated as sufficient proof of who that person is or what someone else should choose for them. The final test is whether a person can produce another response without requiring finished evidence to authorize it.

### Ending Intent

Vesper's extraction succeeds. Rain knows the completed adventure as accurate fact, but he does not feel that he literally lived it. Remembering does not complete him, certify his growth, or reconnect him to a finished self.

Rain also remains publicly a fugitive. He boards an outbound train because leaving is necessary and because he chooses a first destination, a new adventure, and the next step toward his dreams. The final choice matters without vindication, certainty, or recovered ownership of the past.

### Title and Final Line

The title points backward toward records and memory while the ending points beyond what any finished record can contain. Vesper remembers Rain's completed past more completely than Rain experiences it himself; he still does not possess Rain's future. Rain the weather remains movement without moral purity: nourishing, inconvenient, beautiful, exhausting, and destructive.

The locked ending is a present claim, not discovery of a true self:

> I dreamed and dreamed. Now I fall.
>
> In spite of it all, I am Rain.
