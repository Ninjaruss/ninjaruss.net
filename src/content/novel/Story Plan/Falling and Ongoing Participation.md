# Falling and Ongoing Participation

Working refinement · 2026-11-27

The chosen direction is that falling means ongoing participation in life. A committed decision is one moment within it. Rain's name is integral to that meaning.

The blockquotes below preserve the author's words from this conversation and source notes. The connecting first-person prose is an editorial synthesis written for review, not a verbatim statement by the author. Earlier notes remain intact, including their unresolved tensions. The interpretations of the name and climax are proposed expressions of the chosen direction, not additional plot decisions.

## What I want falling to mean

> It isn't guaranteed, but simply being within the act itself is the core.

[C1]

I want falling to include what happens while I am actually involved: trying something I care about, encountering what I did not anticipate, being changed, and deciding how to respond. Making the decision does not finish that process. Neither does discovering that the result hurts or that I cannot do everything I hoped.

Experience matters because it can change what I notice, what I value, and what I become willing or able to attempt. I may recognize a turning point through acting. I do not need an accumulated amount of experience that certifies I am ready. Commitment can emerge within participation, and participation continues after commitment.

## The difficulty I keep returning to

> Almost as if I need an entire thesis or provable valid set of reasons before I can actually commit to a specific goal.

[N1]

The difficulty is not that I have no desires or have never acted. It is that I can turn the next action into a question about whether I am justified, capable, or becoming the person I hoped to be. Reflection can help me choose, but it can also postpone the experience that would let me discover something.

> Even though I do things that progressed towards my dreams, they would either not be completed or were put off after a period of time.

[N3]

Those efforts remain real. Leaving a path, learning, making something, and trying again do not stop mattering because they failed to produce a complete transformation. Rain can have made consequential choices and still find the next one difficult.

> I still had to really push through and make it a reality. And now it is.

[N4]

I want his self-doubt to leave room for readers to recognize efforts he cannot consistently appreciate. His fear of being ordinary can complicate his desire to discover his capabilities without becoming the explanation for everything he does.

## The value of being within the act

> I used to have that, I guess, carefree feeling of working on something that matters to me, rather than what could this thing bring to me, like some sort of, I guess, monetary gain or just any guarantee afterwards.

[N2]

I still want to create something meaningful. I do not want every experience to have to prove that I will accomplish something extraordinary before it can matter. Sometimes I discover what matters through the attempt itself. The outcome can inform what I do next without becoming a final judgment of my worth.

This also means keeping the value of an experience distinct from the wisdom of every decision within it. Feeling alive can be real while I have made a mistake, frightened someone, or incurred a cost I need to answer for. Responsibility includes listening, recognizing a limit, and changing my approach.

## Choosing a road and wanting to be understood

> the path to that good life that I want for myself requires sacrifice and risk while pursuing the road that I must make for myself

[C3]

I want room to discover a life through choices whose results I cannot promise in advance. I also want the people I care about to understand why those choices matter. These wants can pull against each other.

> I felt so torn of telling them even though I already made the decision.

[C4]

Making a decision privately and acknowledging it openly can be separate difficulties. Concealment may protect a fragile decision for a time while creating another consequence around trust.

> It was slightly my intention, but I really felt as if I couldn't have the courage to tell them

[C5]

For Rain, this is a tension to dramatize, not a reason to make his family automatically wrong. Respecting his agency does not require agreeing with every action. Accepting criticism does not require accepting someone else's complete definition of his future. The emotional experience can inform the theft story without making theft and quitting a job equivalent actions.

## What I am releasing

> The real solution is to take action and keep working to prove I'm not wrong

[N5]

That older wish to prove myself is part of the material. The refined direction does not pretend it has disappeared. It asks whether I can continue pursuing what matters when the outcome cannot promise understanding or vindication.

What I am trying to release is the demand that experience settle who I am before I respond again. I can carry the past, learn from it, and remain responsible for it without requiring it to guarantee a particular future. Accepting help can be part of this. So can resting, refusing, renewing the same aim, or recognizing that an aim needs to change.

Falling is therefore not a demand for constant movement, greater risk, or more suffering. The question is how I participate within the conditions and capacities I actually have. A quiet life can be genuinely chosen. A difficult life does not automatically prove courage or growth.

## Why Rain's name matters

> The second is my direction of what I want falling to mean and why the name Rain is integral to the story

[C2]

Proposed expression: Rain does not have to become somebody else before he can live. He is already encountering the world and being affected by it, including while he hesitates. The name can carry that continuing relationship with reality through the whole story, instead of naming a quality he earns at the end.

Rain the weather falls within conditions it does not choose. Rain the person has agency within conditions he cannot fully control. The metaphor should preserve that distinction: it does not make his decisions automatic, turn him into pure momentum, or make the weather a measure of his virtue.

The opening's inability to fall can express how he experiences his own stuckness. The ending's “Now I fall” can express present willingness without denying every earlier act or promising that he will never hesitate again.

## What the climax tests

The current climax preserves the detailed record of Rain's adventure while removing felt ownership of having chosen it. He returns able to respond, relapses, and eventually takes the consequential position himself. The final train remains a chosen direction without public vindication or restored ownership of his past. These are existing story commitments, not changes proposed here. [S1]

The metaphor's test is whether he can remain involved without the reassuring experience of his past certifying what kind of person he has become. His history still mattered. Its meaning and influence do not make the present response for him. The ending can continue falling rather than announce that he has finally reached the right level of growth.

The outline's claim that every previous decision avoided consequence remains a separate consistency issue. This document does not adopt it or silently repair it. Likewise, it does not settle the Ghost's exact contribution to commitment, the surrender conditions, or Shiori's revisions.

## What this asks of the writing

> Obviously, a change like this will not solve my issues, but I believe that it will give me greater opportunity to challenge myself without anything else holding me back.

[N7]

Let consequential decisions change circumstances without instantly changing the whole person. Let ordinary experience, connection, help, disappointment, and a revised aim matter. Show which particular action Rain postpones or takes, what he encounters, and how that encounter changes his next response. He does not need to explain the thesis in dialogue.

> I'd like to say I do that every single day, but really I just do a small thing to progress towards my goals bit by bit
> Attempting to learn a new word, trying to write another thought out

[N6]

This is a working direction for living and writing, not another standard I must satisfy before I am allowed to begin. The next attempt can be small. Its importance may become visible only later.
