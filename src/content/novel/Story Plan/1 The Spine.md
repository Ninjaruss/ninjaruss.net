\#\#\# Arc 3 · The Prisoner

\*\*Choice pressure:\*\* Rain can remain inside a possible life with no outside consequences or end even the beloved life. His choice sends the Ghost toward Roxana, whose acceptance of help creates the escape.

\- \*\*1 · Intake\*\* — The Pursuer completes forms while Rain's goggles, notebook, and Roxana's lighter are logged into a tray. Roxana is lowered into a pod. Rain goes last. The tank, not a guard, is the lock.

\- \*\*2 · True silence\*\* — Sensory deprivation removes time, gravity, and orientation as lived experiences. Rain enters the white void and is met by the suit-self: a replica of himself wearing the life he abandoned, the suit and the polish and the ease. It accuses him of squandering what he had. He readies an answer; it fades before he can give one, and he calls it a coward for leaving. The suit-self is the Split, wearing a life's shape and not yet marked.

\- \*\*3 · The lives\*\* — Time dilation lets Rain live possible lives whole. The first is fully rendered; later lives compress from scenes to paragraphs to lines, because each keeps the shape of the last while promising more. In every life the Split meets him again wearing that life's Rain; he does not yet know it for the same speaker. They feel real but cannot acquire consequences outside themselves.

\- \*\*A life · 1–3\*\* — Optional, self-contained lives. They reveal Rain and cannot contain plot facts required by the spine.

\- \*\*4 · The mountain\*\* — The beloved life comes last, lasts longest, and is genuinely happy. Rain ends it because a preserved possibility cannot acquire consequences beyond itself, not because it was false or hid a truer self. The mountain is the figure of what he has relinquished: a copy of himself for every life he lived and left, and the beloved life would add one more. Seeing them is the recognition — no one in the dream has ever answered him but himself, which is why nothing here can accrue. The Ghost is expelled toward Roxana while Rain remains unaware. The Split receives its first distinct mark and stops wearing a life's shape.

\- \*\*5 · The hand\*\* — The only external POV cut. The Pursuer reviews the record of the person Roxana failed to protect, outside her pod. Empathic Burn has given Roxana nothing to work on: everyone near her is suppressed, so she has no one to feel and no one to need her. Then Rain ends the beloved life, and the change crosses — she registers him, and knows she is not alone. The Ghost offers an open hand that asks nothing. Roxana accepts. A raw spark passes through the scars and burns through both seals.

\- \*\*6 · The carry\*\* — Rain's pod opens as Roxana collapses. They recover the Imprints from intake. Rain carries her out. Real rain and the Circuit Line wait outside.

\#\#\# Arc 5 · The Proof

\- \*\*3 · The rearguard\*\* — Roxana holds the last gate against the Pursuer. She loses exchanges, reaches the choice to stop, and re-enters. He can only continue the old burn. He spends ahead of schedule, empties, and stops walking. He does not ally; the gate is simply no longer manned.

\- \*\*4 · The overwatch\*\* — Claire alternates the broad prediction of Visual Calculus with Convergence's local continuous revision while coordinating through Shiori's feeds. Complexity, duration, execution, and repeated use overwhelm her. The ribbon dies, range collapses, and the lens comes off. At contact range, without a complete model, she says \*Go\*.

\- \*\*5 · The extraction\*\* — Fold the climb and Shiori's feeds opening the route into the opening. \*\*Open with the Perfect Clone overwhelming Vesper\*\* — it works for one reason, that he has not yet committed to reading it, and an intangible counterfeit simulating impact is unanswerable until you are willing to walk through it. Rain is winning with what \`Themes and Motifs\` calls “perfected stasis, not growth,” and the extraction is the bill for it. Then: Vesper reads Rain through Roxana's finished record. Perfect Clone has worked effectively throughout the approach, but repeated use has compounded the friction between Rain's decision and his real body's initiation. He knows the correct response and begins it too late. Direct contact succeeds and the UI, music, and world are removed with \*\*every decision he ever made\*\*. Claire and Roxana arrive spent. Do not interrupt or soften the extraction: Vesper takes 100% of the target and receives the finished past in full.

\- \*\*6 · The void\*\* — Only \*you\* remains. The record is whole and vivid — he can describe the room where he met Roxana, her hands, what she said — and there is no moment in any of it where he chose anything. He is reading his own story, not remembering living it. Arc 3 and the Perfect Clone are why he recognises the state rather than why he survives it. Non-branching choices create present consequences without restoring ownership or reconstructing an old identity. The interface and music rebuild through continued response.

\- \*\*7 · The return\*\* — Rain returns capable of responding, not newly completed. \*\*He reaches for the Perfect Clone\*\* — the move that has worked all arc — and Vesper, holding every decision Rain ever made, is ready for it. The counterfeit was always a bluff that force passes through; Vesper ignores it, reaches the real body, and Rain is badly hurt. His relapse is used against him by the man carrying the complete record of it. The card ends on \*\*the smile\*\*: the asylum patient's expression from Arc 2, arriving now with everything still in it. Vesper has manufactured that face by subtraction many times and has never once seen it arrive on its own. \*(Revised 2026-11-05 — this supplies the injury the card previously assumed without cause.)\*
