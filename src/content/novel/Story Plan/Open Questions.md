## Open Questions

These points are intentionally unresolved. Do not treat earlier speculative wording as canon.

- Did the asylum predate Vesper? Is he its operator, public face, research subject, or something else?
- Where within the five years do his mother's Melt, Shiori's extraction, the unknown Melt, Aster's death, and the Widower's nine treatments fall?
- What institution allowed a thirteen-to-eighteen-year-old Vesper to practice, and what did it believe it was authorizing?

### Melt details

- What exact autobiographical memory was destroyed in each of the three Melts?
- Which procedural or behavioral residue remains from each?
- Which foreign behavior belongs to an intact shard Vesper is consciously consulting, and which belongs to a Melt he cannot remember?
- What does Roxana understand and consent to before her extraction, and what information is withheld?

### Character history

- Is Roxana's childhood fire primarily the origin of her providing or a debt she believes she must keep paying?
- What single specific act by Aster should define the person Roxana is trying to rebuild and Vesper is trying to use?
- What did Vesper remove from the Gamer, and what ordinary asylum task makes the loss visible without explanation?

### Magic-system edges

- How do living and non-object substrates retain or release resonance on the page?
- Can the same Imprint resonate strongly with more than one person, and what happens to an existing bond?
- How much can a passive physically manifest before an Imprint without becoming a repeatable ability?

### Scene content

- Which optional lives appear in Arc 3, and which one is the beloved life?
- What exact first destination has Rain chosen on the outbound train?
- Which public account leaves Rain a fugitive after the correction apparatus is broken?

The endpoint is locked even while those details remain open: the train is the final scene, Rain is still publicly a fugitive, and he leaves toward a new adventure because he has chosen to begin taking steps toward his dreams.
