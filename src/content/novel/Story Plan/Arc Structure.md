\#\# Arc Structure

This file records what each arc does to the story. \`1 The Spine.md\` owns scene order and causal detail; \`Choice Points.md\` owns pitfall behavior.

\#\#\# Arc Function

\| Arc \| Central question \| Thematic development \| Handoff \|

\|---\|---\|---\|---\|

\| \*\*1 · Fugitive\*\* \| Can you take one irreversible step before feeling ready? \| \*\*Action produces consequence.\*\* Rain acts and his life becomes worse; risk is not automatic reward. \| The apparent refuge is pleasant and open-doored. \|

\| \*\*2 · Mirror\*\* \| Can you keep wanting when wanting hurts? \| \*\*Relief can genuinely help, but successful intervention can begin replacing participation.\*\* The danger is not treatment or repetition by itself; it is the expanding authority to determine what the person may become next. \| Rain and Roxana are referred to the state. \|

\| \*\*3 · Prisoner\*\* \| Can you end every life you could have lived, even the one where you were happy? \| \*\*Possibility itself has value.\*\* Rain leaves the beloved life because preserved possibility cannot acquire consequences beyond itself, not because it was false. \| Rain carries Roxana toward the Circuit Line. \|

\| \*\*4 · Witness\*\* \| Can you choose when nothing is chasing you? \| \*\*Choice does not validate itself.\*\* Rain refuses a defensible amnesty and still loses his home. \| The Perfect Clone carries the group toward the caldera under false progress. \|

\| \*\*5 · Proof\*\* \| Can you choose without knowing who that choice proves you are? \| \*\*Evidence reaches its limit.\*\* Vesper owns the complete past record and still cannot determine the next response. \| Rain remains a fugitive and boards the outbound train by choice. \|

\#\#\# What Each Arc Leaves Unresolved

From \`Research/Seed/Rain - Philosophical Seed\` §8. The ladder is why the five arcs do not repeat one lesson in escalating circumstances: each one settles something and exposes the next problem.

\| Arc \| Establishes \| Leaves unresolved, which the next arc takes up \|

\|---\|---\|---\|

\| \*\*1 · Fugitive\*\* \| Making a decision means allowing it real consequences. Acting is not rewarded. \| If pursuing something can hurt this much, why keep wanting it? \|

\| \*\*2 · Mirror\*\* \| Relieving suffering matters, and relief becomes dangerous when it removes a person's capacity to participate in their own future. \| Why choose an uncertain reality when a more comfortable alternative is genuinely available? \|

\| \*\*3 · Prisoner\*\* \| Rain can relinquish valuable possibilities and return to the reality he actually inhabits. \| Does understanding what he needs to do mean he can actually do it? \|

\| \*\*4 · Witness\*\* \| Internal understanding has to become actual participation, while the hesitation, insecurity and uncertainty remain. \| Can he continue without an idealised identity, or accumulated proof that he is becoming the person he wants to be? \|

\| \*\*5 · Proof\*\* \| Rain's completed history matters and is not the authority over his next response. \| A life that is still unfinished. Nothing resolves it, and nothing should. \|

Arc 3's rows and the seed differ in emphasis rather than substance, and both are intended: the table above names what Rain gives up — possibility has value, which is why ending it costs — and the seed names what he gains by ending it.

Arc 4's \*\*Choice does not validate itself\*\* is the seed's §6 ethical boundary: “making a decision doesn't automatically make that decision good… so do the consequences of our choices for other people.” It is currently asserted here and dramatised nowhere. The amnesty refusal in \`Arc 4 · 8\` costs Rain his home station, which the correction was going to take regardless, so he pays nothing for the refusal he was not already paying. \`\{TK\}\` whether the refusal binds someone else on that train — whose cost, and whether Rain ever learns of it. Until that lands, arc 4 establishes the seed's understanding-into-participation and not its ethical limit. See \`docs/remember-rain/2026-11-05-philosophical-seed-review.md\` §3.

\#\#\# Arc 5 Convergence

Arc 5 is one convergence, not an ensemble climax followed by a second ending. Roxana, Claire, and Shiori are tested while clearing and maintaining the path toward Vesper; their actions make Rain's extraction possible rather than preventing it.

Extraction is the hinge. It is followed, in order, by the void, Rain's return, his refusal to remove the Split, the apparatus breaking, and brief clean-rain actions for the ensemble. The outbound train remains the final scene; nothing follows it.
